athlete, sport or news no results found. 21.07.2026 yeti announced as official supplier for team england ahead of glasgow 2026 team england is delighted to welcome yeti as an official supplier ahead of the glasgow 2026 commonwealth games. yeti, the premium outdoor brand is known for its durable, high-performance drinkware, coolers, and gear. through this collaboration, yeti will supply all team england athletes with co-branded rambler bottles, supporting their hydration needs in training, travel, and competition. the partnership also supports team england's commitment to sustainability by encouraging the use of reusable bottles and helping to reduce single-use plastic consumption across athlete programmes and events. mark osikoya, ceo of commonwealth games england, said: " we're delighted to welcome yeti as an official supplier. yeti's products are known for their exceptional quality, and providing our athletes with bottles built to last aligns perfectly with our performance standards and our sustainability goals." david heath, managing director of yeti emea, said: "our products are designed to perform in exceptional circumstances. we're proud to support a team that does the same, and look forward to seeing team england give it their all on one of the most prestigious sporting stages in the world." from early-morning training sessions to the biggest moments of competition, yeti will support team england athletes every step of the way as they prepare for glasgow 2026. share facebook twitter see all news related news see all news 22.10.2026 team england
