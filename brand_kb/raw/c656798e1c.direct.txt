"this has been an extremely successful tournament by every metric," pga tour ceo brian rolapp said. "our members love it. travelers is an amazing partner. they do a lot of things that we're looking for in a championship series event." championship series the travelers championship, a signature event for the past three years, is the first announced for the tour's overhauled schedule. the schedule will divide competition into two tiers: the championship series and the challenger series. the championship series will feature golf's biggest stars competing throughout the season, including the four majors, postseason events, and team competitions such as the ryder cup. the #travelerschamp has been officially named to the @pgatour championship series, coming in 2028. 🏆 excited for what the future holds. pic.twitter.com/i8auifj669 - travelerschamp (@travelerschamp) july 27, 2026 the pga tour will announce the full 2028 schedule at a later date. "our goal every year is for the travelers championship to be one of the best tournaments on the pga tour," said travelers executive vice president and chief administrative officer andy bessette. "being part of the upcoming pga tour championship series is validation of the collective effort that travelers, the tournament team, fans and volunteers have invested over the past two decades." fan favorite usa today named the tournament "best golf tournament for fans" earlier this year and newsweek 's fans choice awards named it "best pga tour tournament event" in 2025. the announcement follows a milestone year for the tournament. the 2026 travelers championship marked 20 years with travelers as title sponsor. "this partnership with travelers and with the pga tour has taken the tournament to incredible new heights." travelers championship's nathan grube in that time, the tournament has generated more than $40 million for more than 1,000 charities. in 2026 alone, the tournament generated a record $5 million for more than 270 charities. "this partnership with travelers and with the pga tour has taken the tournament to incredible new heights," said travelers championship director nathan grube. "we're proud of the travelers championship, the economic impact it makes on our community and the state of connecticut, and the hundreds of local charities that continue to benefit financially."
