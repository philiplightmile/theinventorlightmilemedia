craft technologies joins sammy smith as primary partner in 2026 by speedway digest staff may 5, 2026 reading time: 2 mins read 0 rsmg announced today that alpha craft technologies, a full-service, women-owned electrical contractor, will serve as a primary partner for driver sammy smith in multiple races during the 2026 season, beginning may 23 at charlotte motor speedway on board the no. 8 jr motorsports chevrolet in the nascar o'reilly auto parts series. the partnership expands alpha craft technologies' presence in motorsports and aligns the company with one of the sport's rising drivers. smith will debut the alpha craft technologies branding at charlotte as part of the multi-race agreement. alpha craft technologies joins an established group of partners supporting smith, including pilot company, tmc transportation, allstate peterbilt group, the renda group, and sinclair tractors. "aligning with rsmg and partnering with sammy smith for a multi-race program in 2026 marks a significant milestone for alpha craft technologies," said alpha craft technologies."motorsports is one of the most demanding and data-driven environments in sports, making it an ideal platform for our brand. we're excited to partner with someone who shares our commitment to performance, precision, and innovation." related posts timberland pro 200 results from phoenix raceway timberland pro 200 starting lineup at phoenix raceway championship pressure builds ahead of nascar o'reilly series race at phoenix raceway "i'm happy to have alpha craft technologies join me as a primary partner for multiple races this season beginning with my o'reilly series car with jrm at charlotte," said smith.
