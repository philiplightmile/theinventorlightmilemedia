"this is a huge moment in the history of corr, and we couldn't be more excited to enter this next chapter with amsoil," said corr co-owner jason ramsay. "amsoil has been a major supporter of off-road racing for decades, especially short course racing, and their product is already in many of the vehicles on our starting line. having their name on the series means a tremendous amount to us, and we're excited about what we can build together." "amsoil has a long history of supporting short-course off-road and we're eager to be part of the action in the southwest. corr is creating new opportunities for racers of all ages and experience levels in a family-friendly, competitive environment. that's important for the future of off-road motorsports, so we're excited to support their efforts," said sara oettinger, amsoil marketing manager. for more information about championship off-road racing, visit www.corracing.com . for more information about amsoil, visit www.amsoil.com . additional announcements about the 2026-27 corr presented by amsoil season will be made in the coming weeks. about amsoil amsoil formulates advanced lubricants and performance products for those who demand the best. amsoil products are trusted worldwide in automotive, heavy-duty, powersports, industrial and racing applications. for more information or to order, contact your nearest amsoil dealer or visit amsoil.com . amsoil inc., 925 tower ave. superior, wi 54880. about championship off-road racing championship off-road racing is a short-course off-road racing series featuring professional, amateur and youth competitors at venues across arizona, nevada and california. corr is committed to delivering competitive racing, creating opportunities for racers at every level and growing the sport of short-course off-road racing for competitors, partners and fans. recent news corr presented by amsoil registration open now for november 19-22 r-n-r steel firebird desert open media credentials open for 2026-27 corr presented by amsoil season corr spotlight: 200 limited - corr's newest youth class corr presented by amsoil adds pro spec trucks for 2026-27 season lines freely. adding/removing a whole ?
