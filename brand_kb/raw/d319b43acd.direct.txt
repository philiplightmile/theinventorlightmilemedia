(michelin) is recalling certain agilis cross climate c-metric tires, size 185/60r15c. chunks of tread rubber may detach from the shoulder blocks. as such, these tires fail to comply with the requirements of federal motor vehicle safety standard number 139, "new pneumatic radial tires for light vehicles." the risk missing rubber chunks can cause a tire failure, increasing the risk of a crash. the fix michelin will inspect and replace the tires as necessary, free of charge. owner notification letters were mailed july 3, 2025. owners may contact michelin's customer service at 1-888-971-3801. affected vehicles michelin agilis crossclimate c-metric source: nhtsa recall database · campaign 25t008000 recall snapshot campaign 25t008000 reported may 19, 2025 vehicles affected 6,888 component tires › tread/belt owners notified jul 3, 2025 reported by mfr source nhtsa check your vin enter your 17-character vin to see every open nhtsa recall on your exact vehicle. check a vehicle vin browse recalls latest recalls do not drive recalls all makes & models recall guides how us vehicle recalls work what to do if your car is recalled recall vs technical service bulletin © 2026 motor verso. recall data sourced from nhtsa. privacy contact all recalls
