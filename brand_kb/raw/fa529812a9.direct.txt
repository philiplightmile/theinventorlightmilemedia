presenting sponsor for tsport 200 in 2026 by speedway digest staff april 13, 2026 reading time: 1 min read 0 lucas oil indianapolis raceway park and tsport trucks have announced that warn industries has signed on as presenting sponsor of the nascar craftsman truck series race scheduled for july 24th, part of the highly anticipated nascar doubleheader event. warn industries, a leader in durable, innovative, and trusted off-road products since 1948, will partner with tsport as presenting sponsor for the first time at the 2026 nascar craftsman truck series race at lucas oil indianapolis raceway park. the event will remain a 137.2-mile, 200-lap race and will now be officially branded as the tsport 200 presented by warn. "we are excited to have warn industries join our nascar truck event as presenting sponsor. the warn brand is a natural fit for this event and our customers." rollie miller, general manager for lucas oil indianapolis raceway park. "we're excited to partner with tsport as the presenting race sponsor. coming fresh off some big off-road race wins this year; this will be a tremendous opportunity for us to market the warn brand to nascar race fans." jake petersen, vp of product & marketing for the warn group. related posts firestone grand prix of st. petersburg tickets go on sale tomorrow jason derulo to headline nascar championship weekend at homestead-miami speedway 2026 lincoln speedway night of champions info released tickets, parking, and camping for the nascar doubleheader at lucas oil indianapolis raceway park-featuring the arca menards series liuna 150 and the nascar craftsman truck series tsport 200 presented by warn-are now available. fans can purchase at www.raceirp.com/nascar or by calling (317) 969-8600. irp pr share tweet speedway digest staff follow us on twitter @speedwaydigest related posts speedway news firestone grand prix of st. petersburg tickets go on sale tomorrow october 22, 2026 speedway news jason derulo to headline nascar championship weekend at homestead-miami speedway october 22, 2026 speedway news 2026 lincoln speedway night of champions info released october 22, 2026 speedway news stem day presented by first bank of alabama returns for fall race weekend at talladega superspeedway october 21, 2026 speedway news all dirt roads lead to charlotte for dave smith motors world of outlaws world finals nov. 4-7 october 21, 2026 speedway news monster truck bash returns to all american speedway this saturday october 24th october 21, 2026 no result view all result talladega -º - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - talladega weather copyright © 2010 - 2026 sts motorsports inc. all rights reserved. navigate site advertise privacy policy editorial guidelines sd staff writer login follow us welcome back! login to your account below remember me forgotten password?
