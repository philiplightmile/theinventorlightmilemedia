ai insights top shop webinars digital magazine news yokohama official tire for two major tracks yokohama is now the official tire of both sebring international raceway and road atlanta. by tire review staff published: march 17, 2008 yokohama tire corp. signed a three-year deal to sponsor the tracks. as part of the deal, yokohama signage will be placed in key areas of both tracks, including the crossover bridges and other camera-view areas. yokohama also plans dealer tie-ins in each local market prior to major events and hospitality programs. yokohama is already a sponsor of mazda raceway laguna seca in monterey, calif.
