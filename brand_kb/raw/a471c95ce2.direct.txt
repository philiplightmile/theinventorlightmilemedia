"darlington raceway is the track too tough to tame, so partnering with a brand like dead on tools known for making strong, innovative products is a great combination," said darlington raceway president kerry tharp. "we look forward to welcoming dead on tools as part of the darlington raceway family for both nascar cup series race weekends this season." dead on tools is an entitlement partner at martinsville speedway for the dead on tools 250, which is the penultimate nascar xfinity series playoff race, on saturday, oct. 30. dead on tools is also the official hand tool & tool belt of martinsville speedway. "we could not be more thrilled to partner with the track too tough to tame," said brian ranallo, marketing manager for dead on tools.
