mossberg reaffirms commitment as longbeard sponsor for nwtf convention and sport show - the national wild turkey federation skip to content menu lifestyle hub events resources find a chapter return back home page shop join/renew donate press releases mossberg reaffirms commitment as longbeard sponsor for nwtf convention and sport show edgefield, s.c. - mossberg, a trusted partner of the national wild turkey federation since 2008, is returning as a longbeard sponsor for the 2025 nwtf convention and sport show, scheduled for feb. 12-16 in nashville, tennessee. january 21, 2025 2 min read "we've enjoyed a lengthy and prosperous collaboration with nwtf, and we are delighted to extend this partnership into 2025 as a longbeard sponsor," said john maclellan, vice president of sales and marketing at mossberg. "we can't wait to get back to nashville to re-connect with our nwtf family and friends!" as a trailblazer in the firearms industry, mossberg has been a consistent supporter of nwtf's mission and events. at the 2025 convention and sport show, mossberg will co-sponsor the kickoff party and serve as a supporting sponsor of the waterin' hole. attendees can connect with mossberg representatives and explore their products at booth 1331 in the exhibit hall. "we are excited to once again have mossberg as a key sponsor for our convention," said natalia daniels, nwtf national director of corporate relations and licensing. "their long-standing support has been instrumental in the success of our events, and we value their continued partnership in making the convention and sport show an unforgettable experience for everyone." the nwtf convention and sport show, officially sponsored by mossy oak, kicks off with an exciting concert on the evening of wednesday, feb. 12. the convention officially begins on thursday, feb. 13, with the morning opening of the sport show floor, showcasing over 500 premier exhibitors from across the outdoor industry. the day concludes with the high-energy kickoff party that evening. the sport show runs through saturday, feb. 15, at the gaylord opryland resort and convention center, with the convention wrapping up on sunday morning with a stand-alone, free worship service. for additional details, including the full schedule of events, visit www.nwtf.org/get-involved/nwtf-convention . about the national wild turkey federation since 1973, the national wild turkey federation has invested over half a billion dollars into wildlife conservation and has positively impacted over 23 million acres of critical wildlife habitat. the nwtf has also invested over $10 million into wild turkey research to guide the management of the wild turkey population and to ensure sustainable populations into perpetuity. the organization continues to deliver its mission by working across boundaries on a landscape scale through its four shared values: clean and abundant water, healthy forests and wildlife habitat, resilient communities, and robust recreational opportunities. with the help of its dedicated members, partners and staff, the nwtf continues its work to provide healthy habitats. and healthy harvests. for future generations. about o.f. mossberg & sons, inc. founded in
