(photo courtesy ohio peterbilt) columbus, ohio. - ohio peterbilt is announcing the grand opening celebration of its newest, state-of-the-art full-service dealership in etna, located approximately 20 miles east of downtown columbus. "our etna location is more than just a new dealership, it's a destination," said mike crawford, vice president of ohio peterbilt. celebrate with heavy d and diesel dave the celebration will take place july 29 at 1:00 p.m., and the community is invited to join the festivities. headlining the event are heavy d and diesel dave, stars of the hit television series diesel brothers , who will appear as special guests during the celebration. a select group of customers will have the opportunity to participate in an exclusive meet-and-greet with the well-known duo. enter here for a chance to meet them. "we're excited to officially open our doors and celebrate with our customers, partners and the community," crawford said. "having heavy d and diesel dave join us makes this event even more special, and we look forward to welcoming everyone to see what this facility has to offer." a next level dealership "the new etna location represents a major investment by ohio peterbilt," the company said. "this new etna facility represents the next level of what a peterbilt dealership should be. purpose-built for productivity, efficiency, and uptime, making it one of the most advanced full-service peterbilt locations in the nation. the 82,000-square-foot facility features a full-service shop with 25 service bays, a central parts distribution center, expansive office and retail space, and driver-focused amenities designed to keep fleets moving and reduce downtime. with convenient access near the i-70 corridor, the etna dealership is positioned to better serve customers throughout central ohio and the midwest." for more information about the etna location or the grand opening celebration, click here. dana guthrie dana guthrie is an award-winning journalist who has been featured in multiple newspapers, books and magazines across the globe. tags featured , primary feature post navigation previous article ftr: preliminary net trailer orders down in april next article pickup driver critical after head-on crash with semi related articles more from author trucking industry joins federal push to strengthen human trafficking detection new york police make 200 dwi arrests over columbus day weekend cbp finds nearly 1,600 pounds of meth in tomatillo shipment truckstop, ftr: spot rates once again barely budge in the latest week dat: load posts dip 3% while linehaul rates hold firm act: capacity tightening early ahead of holiday season dana guthrie dana guthrie is an award-winning journalist who has been featured in multiple newspapers, books and magazines across the globe. for over 30 years, the objective of the trucker editorial team has been to produce content focused on truck drivers that is relevant, objective and engaging. after reading this article, feel free to leave a comment about this article or the topics covered in this article for the author or the other readers to enjoy. let them know what you think!
