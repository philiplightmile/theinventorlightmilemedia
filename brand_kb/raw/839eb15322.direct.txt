building on its legacy of agriculture leadership, case ih is enhancing its comprehensive lineup of equipment and technology designed to meet the evolving needs of modern farmers. from the introduction of its most powerful tractor yet, the steiger ® 785 quadtrac ® , to the return of the farmall ® m and more, case ih is advancing its heritage of power, performance and innovation. this week, the brand is unveiling a wide range of solutions at farm progress show designed to maximize uptime, simplify operations and improve overall productivity and profitability. "we've taken some of our most iconic series and given them even more power and precision, all in service of meeting the needs of farmers today and in the future," said kurt coffey, case ih vice president, north america. "no matter the operation size or fleet mix, case ih is providing solutions that help operators do more with less." at the 2025 farm progress show, case ih is displaying a wide range of solutions: steiger 785 quadtrac brings more horsepower with purpose the new steiger 785 quadtrac is the most powerful tractor in the case ih lineup. delivering 785-rated/853-peak horsepower*, the latest model of the celebrated steiger series is built to maximize power to the ground while maintaining superior traction and minimal compaction. its higher horsepower allows farmers to handle larger implements or pull the same implements even faster. learn more about the highest horsepower steiger here . more solutions for livestock producers case ih is debuting several new tractor models in the farmall series , a trusted staple for generations, which remains a cornerstone of the brand's commitment to dependability and versatility. the highly-anticipated farmall 120 super m , with a long heritage of high performance and reliability, delivers increased productivity with seamlessly integrated technology and a premium operator comfort, making it the go-to choice for demanding tasks. the new lineup also includes the farmall c , which prioritizes utility and operator comfort, offering exceptional flexibility for a variety of applications. the farmall a is a lightweight, agile tractor that now includes higher horsepower, providing an economical and efficient solution for both seasonal and year-round tasks. for baling needs, the new rb6 series variable chamber round baler delivers consistent, high-quality results through upgraded durability and integrated precision technology. designed with operator ease in mind, the rb6 series improves productivity while meeting the demands of busy operations. learn more about these product launches here . harvesting solutions that maximize efficiency the robust case ih harvesting lineup offers a combine for every producer. onsite at farm progress show, case ih is displaying the af10 as well as the axial-flow® 260 series combines . paired seamlessly with durable c500 series corn heads , these combines reduce losses while improving reliability and ease of operation. also on display is the new fd2 plus flexdraper head by macdon, offering farmers an advanced, flexible header that hugs the ground tightly, reaching deeper into low spots to scoop out every last bit of available crop. visitors are invited to learn about the variety of precision technology and automation features on case ih combines, which take the guesswork out of harvesting. updates in application and tillage solutions case ih is expanding its senseapply™ technology - launched earlier this year as a factory fit option - with the senseapply kit, a retrofit option compatible with select model year 2023 and newer sprayers. this kit brings variable rate application and selective spraying capabilities to existing sprayers, helping operators maximize the output of every input. additionally on display is the patriot ® 50 series sprayer , built for precise product application and maximum uptime. featuring an all-aluminum boom with factory-installed boom recirculation, a new auto rinse system, display and fill station updates, the patriot sprayer ensures efficient product use and effective application across the field. onsite, attendees can also learn more about updates in soil command™ tillage technology, including the new seedbed sense speed control, available with the tiger-mate™ 255 field cultivator. an industry first, the seedbed sense speed control proactively adjusts the tractor's speed to optimize productivity without sacrificing the quality of the seedbed. technology that drives performance case ih continues to strengthen its farmer-first innovations through advanced precision technologies and ai-driven solutions, designed to make operations more efficient, productive and profitable. operators can take advantage of continued enhancements to fieldops™, a comprehensive platform connecting all aspects of farming operations. new features such as ai boundary creation allow operators to quickly generate field boundaries using satellite imagery and machine learning, saving valuable time and effort. after launching last year on all mobile devices, fieldops is now available on ipads and offers larger screen visibility, faster load times and the ability to view more data at once, enhancing ease of use. operators can also take advantage of integrated precision technology offerings from case ih - such as connectivity included and fieldops - with technology packages available to meet individual needs. technology packages are now available on model year 2026 steiger, magnum™, optum ® , select puma ® model tractors and larger with pro 1200 display, 260 series and af series combines and patriot 50 series sprayers. driving uptime with customer services case ih is using artificial intelligence to reduce customer downtime with tools such as the new visual search tool , which lets farmers and dealers identify replacement parts simply by uploading a photo, eliminating delays caused by missing part numbers. case ih is also supporting uptime through its max protect package for model year 2026 optum series tractors. max protect helps drive continuous support through extended warranties, planned maintenance agreements, uptime service assistance and connectivity. visit case ih at farm progress show farm progress show visitors are invited to view the latest product launches and
