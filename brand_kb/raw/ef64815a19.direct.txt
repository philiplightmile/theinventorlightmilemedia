presents a donation to the indy public safety foundation in indianapolis on aug. 4, 2026, ahead of a national night out event. adt joined neighbors, first responders and community leaders at national night out events across the country. each year, the event gives neighborhoods a chance to come together and get to know the people who help keep them safe. this marked the fourth consecutive year that adt sponsored national night out. adt team members participated in hundreds of local events nationwide, sharing safety tips and engaging with neighbors about ways to help protect their homes and families. through adt safe places , our corporate giving program, the company committed $75,000 in support of first responders and community organizations. from south florida to suburban chicago, adt leaders joined national night out events to present donations and recognize the people and organizations making a difference in their communities. "communities are stronger when neighbors and first responders come together, and that's why we're proud to once again support national night out," said kim miller, chief marketing & communications officer at adt. "helping provide peace of mind is at the heart of what we do, and it was an honor to recognize the people and organizations dedicated to keeping their neighborhoods safe." adt employees distribute free backpacks to children during a national night out event in indianapolis on aug. 4, 2026. in indianapolis, adt presented a $10,000 donation to the indy public safety foundation. "this donation helps us continue our work to enhance public safety and quality of life in indianapolis," said dane nutty, president and ceo of the indy public safety foundation. "we appreciate adt's support and commitment to our community." across the country, adt team members took part in the festivities alongside neighbors and first responders. "when you see families walking around like this, it brings a smile to your face," said joe bonilla, an adt solutions advisor who participated in a national night out event in margate, florida.
