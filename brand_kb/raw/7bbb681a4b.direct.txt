(spectrum news 1/rose todd) akron hosts the northeast regional firefighter challenge for the first time in 20 years by rose todd cleveland published 5:19 am et jul. 24, 2026 published 5:19 am edt jul. 24, 2026 share akron, ohio - more than 100 firefighters are competing in an international competition that puts their strength to the test. it's the northeast regional firefighter challenge. it's an obstacle course that is expected to be done in two minutes or less. it includes five obstacles, and the firefighters are in full firefighter gear. the obstacles are the stair tower climb with high-rise pack, hose hoist, forcible entry, hose advance and victim rescue. "i have 50 pounds of gear on, and then i pick up a 45-pound high-rise pack and run up the stairs," said firefighter david bowman from charlotte, north carolina. "so, there's a total of 95." bowman has been on the job for 29 years. he's competed in the challenge for 28 years, claiming several world championships. he said this challenge makes him a better firefighter but has allowed him to build relationships outside of his department. "i have friends all over the world that i've gotten over the 28 years," said bowman. "i mean, there's people from germany, poland, hungary, canada, all over the world. it's given me the opportunity to go to abu dhabi to compete over there." the challenge started as a federal emergency management agency (fema) funded study in 1976. decades later, it's in five regions with eight competitions a year. this is the first in 20 years akron is hosting the challenge. this is billie johnson's first time competing. he said this challenge was a wakeup call. "i didn't train as i should have for this," said johnson of akron. "i kind of just jumped into it and did it and ended up coming first. so, i'm gonna start to take it more seriously as the years go by. i love that i did it and i want to continue keeping it." johnson has been an akron firefighter for nearly two years. he said in the field, he's part of a team, but in the challenge, he's on his own. "so, it's all up to you but on a regular daily basis you have a team and y'all all work together to get the job done," said johnson. both men and women compete with the same expectations. akron firefighter hunter brancifort said the course isn't easy. "it's definitely challenging but, it's just a good show of you know the physicality we need for our job," said brancifort. while it is a competition, brancifort said everyone supports each other, and she is glad to see the competition being held in akron. "it's just a real good feeling of camaraderie and, you know, coming together, cheering each other on," said brancifort.
