luke bryan farm tour can-am news news enewsletter top stories can-am is official off-road vehicle of luke bryan farm tour alarson september 25, 2018 can-am is the official off-road vehicle of the luke bryan farm tour brp's can-am off-road brand is proud to begin its second season as the official off-road vehicle of the luke bryan farm tour. "we welcome this opportunity to again join luke bryan's passionate cause to support future farmers. can-am is extremely passionate about our products and the consumers who use them, including all those determined farmers in the agricultural community," said ariane boulanger, manager, global marketing - can-am off-road. "we are proud to champion this affection for farmers alongside luke bryan and share the can-am experience with all his dedicated fans." in its second season, the sponsorship affords the can-am team opportunities to meet with more than 100,000 luke bryan farm tour fans. in addition to its official off-road vehicle of farm tour status, can-am also gives live concertgoers the full off-road experience with its bold presence at all six tour stops, complete with a display booth and other exciting happenings. "i can't believe we've been doing this 10 years!" shared luke. "can-am is a passionate brand with incredible off-road products and shares our appreciation for the agricultural community." luke bryan farm tour attendees and country music fans alike can learn about can-am off-road vehicles from local can-am dealership staff and product specialists at designated vehicle display booths at each venue. booth visitors can also register on site for a chance to win a can-am off-road vehicle, including a new outlander xt 650 atv and a xps lubricants cleaning kit. can-am booth activities may include offering free giveaways for those who register to win. the 2018 luke bryan farm tour , celebrating its 10 th year, starts thursday, sept. 27, at ayers family farm in irwin, ohio. it concludes saturday, oct. 6, at doug yates farms, ringgold, ga. four additional farms in the states of illinois, iowa, florida and south carolina make up the other tour stops. related articles opinion: don't let ai become the customer service department october 22, 2026 kawasaki
