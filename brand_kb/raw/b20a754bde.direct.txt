"as our partnership with the nhra continues to grow, we are very excited about the opportunity to become the title sponsor of the nhra u.s. nationals over the next three years," said bob studenic, president and ceo of cornwell quality tools. "we look forward to continuing our support of the nhra and engaging with all the fans during this incredible race weekend." cornwell quality tools will enhance its presence at national events in the nhra mission foods drag racing series, featuring inflatables, signage, display space, pa and jumbotron spots, and sponsorship of the "cornwell quality tools content creator zone." the company will receive television spots and logo integration into all nhra on fox broadcasts, including the cornwell quality tools crew chief confidential segment. at lucas oil indianapolis raceway park, cornwell quality tools will feature permanent signage on the midway arch walking bridge and left lane win light, as well as a suite in the new wally parks tower, scheduled for completion in 2025. "it's a huge thrill to announce cornwell quality tools as our title sponsor for our biggest and most prestigious race, the u.s. nationals," said nhra vice president and chief development officer brad gerber. "their passion and dedication to nhra drag racing makes them the perfect partner for a race that puts our teams and drivers on the grandest stage in our sport." cornwell quality tools also partners with john force racing, which includes several racing champions. top fuel's clay millican, funny car leader austin prock, pro stock's aaron stanfield, and pro stock motorcycle's gaige herrera each secured victories at the big go this season. the nhra u.s. nationals is known for defining moments in nhra history, with wins often establishing legacies for drivers. tony schumacher holds the record with ten victories at the big go, while john force has five funny car wins, tied for the most in class history. bobby bennett thank you for joining us on this journey. your support and trust inspire us every day to deliver the best in drag racing journalism. we are excited about the future and look forward to continuing to serve you with the same dedication and passion that has defined competitionplus.com from the very beginning. all posts share the insights?
