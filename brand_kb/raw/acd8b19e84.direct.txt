(dakota news now) - the cinch playoffs are coming to sioux falls in september. the professional rodeo cowboys association, the state of south dakota, experience sioux falls, and cinch are bringing the cinch playoffs to the city on september 25 through september 27. prca athletes will compete for the governor's cup and a prize of more than 1 million dollars!
