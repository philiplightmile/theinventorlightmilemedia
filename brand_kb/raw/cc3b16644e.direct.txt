shining a light on lineworkers april 17, 2025 • featured april 18 is lineworker appreciation day, a moment to honor the dedicated individuals who keep our communities powered and safe-our incredible lineworkers. from long hours worked on restorations during storm outages to routine maintenance ensuring reliability, our lineworkers are on the front lines, often facing challenging conditions to deliver electricity to our homes and businesses. their expertise and courage often remain behind the scenes, but our customers have stories that highlight the impact these heroes have on their lives. across all seven of our operating utilities, we asked customers to share with us what lineworkers mean to them. the thousands of responses we received did not disappoint. we're proud and thankful that these individuals are part of the aep family, and hope they know how much they mean to the communities they serve. thank you to the crews across our footprint for your commitment, your sacrifice and
