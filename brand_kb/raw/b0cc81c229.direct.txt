work stories case studies news mar 26, 2024 mechanix wear and nascar renew partnership mechanix wear and nascar: a reinforced partnership for performance and safety for over three decades, mechanix wear has been an integral part of nascar, not just as the official glove of nascar, but as a committed partner dedicated to enhancing safety and performance for those who push the limits every weekend. and now, as mechanix wear and nascar renew their storied partnership, they reaffirm a shared commitment to celebrating the spirit of racing and the athletes who fulfill its demands. a glove, a legacy, a passion for motorsport what began with a revolutionary idea-a high-dexterity glove designed to serve as a second skin for the hands of hardcore enthusiasts and professionals alike-has blossomed into a brand that is emblematic of the tenacious spirit of motorsport. mechanix wear's quality isn't just in their products; it's in the dedication they demonstrate to their nascar partners and pit crews, season after season, mile after mile. through this partnership mechanix wear and nascar will continue to tell the story of innovation and protection, weaving together the elements of speed, courage, and cutting-edge technology that make nascar one of the most watched and revered sports in the world. continual enhancement of safety and performance safety is not an occasion, but an ongoing pursuit. mechanix wear, with its endless dedication to the craft, is relentlessly testing, learning, improving. it's a passion shared vividly with nascar, where every move on and off the track is a testament to the dedication of those involved in this high-octane drama. the most valuable pit crew award: a team effort in addition to it's nascar renewal, mechanix wear will continue the most valuable pit crew award program. instituted as a significant acknowledgment of the pit crew's flawless performance, it has become a symbol of recognition and reward, a championship in its own right. the substantial $100,000 award is not just a prize; it's mechanix wear's way of acknowledging the over the wall athletes that help their team succeed. this gesture of appreciation resonates deeply with the nascar ethos, where the pit stop isn't merely a necessity but an art form that fans anticipate and celebrate. shared vision, unified action the partnership renewal signals an exciting chapter for both nascar and mechanix wear. together, they are dedicated to fostering an environment where innovation and safety go hand in hand, much like the drivers and teams that make motorsport roar to life. as part of its partnership, mechanix wear is extending its reach to nascar mexico and nascar canada series. nascar's continued expansion worldwide aligns seamlessly with mechanix wear's mission to provide the best and latest in hand protection gear to all corners of the sport, ensuring global motorsport sets a new safety and performance standard. in the end, as the mechanix wear x nascar partnership continues to set the pace for motorsports safety and performance, the real winners are all those who dare to live life in the fast lane. they're the ones who inspire the story, the drama,
