and chief executive officer of roto-rooter group spencer lee oversees roto-rooter group, which is comprised of roto-rooter services company and roto-rooter corporation. he was born in south korea and immigrated to the united states as a teenager where he became a naturalized u.s. citizen. in july 1980, spencer was hired as assistant to the president of chemed corporation in cincinnati, ohio. months later, chemed acquired roto-rooter corporation, and in 1981, spencer was appointed director of development at roto-rooter. in 1983 he was appointed assistant branch manager of roto-rooter's boston branch. in 1984, spencer was promoted to regional vice president of roto-rooter's mid-atlantic region. in 1996 he became regional vice president of roto-rooter's northeast region. later in 1996, spencer was promoted to senior vice president of operations, a position that took him back to roto-rooter's headquarters in cincinnati. in 1999, he was named chairman and chief executive officer of roto-rooter group. spencer received a bachelor of arts degree in economics from claremont mckenna college in claremont, california. he earned an mba from the university of chicago. robert p. goldschmidt president of roto-rooter group as president of roto-rooter group, robert is responsible for the overall business, including operations, marketing, information technology, national sales, call centers, and corporate mergers and acquisitions. he also provides guidance to roto-rooter's manufacturing division and franchising network. robert joined roto-rooter's parent company, chemed corporation, in 1989 as assistant to the president. in 1992, he was named director of development, where he focused on growing the company via the acquisition of independent roto-rooter franchises. since then, he has completed over one hundred acquisitions. in 1999, robert was promoted to vice president and was tasked with developing and operating a network of corporate call centers. in 2003, he added marketing to his areas of responsibility and was promoted to senior vice president. in 2015, robert was promoted to executive vice president, and in 2020 he was named president of roto-rooter group. robert earned a bachelor of science degree in industrial engineering from georgia tech, and an mba from the kellogg school of management at northwestern university. mark conners executive vice president of contractor operations mark oversees contractor operations at roto-rooter services company. roto-rooter's contractor network serves 69 u.s. markets and 4 canadian markets. mark joined roto-rooter in 1984 as a regional controller. in 1988, he assisted with the expansion of contractor operations, and was subsequently promoted to director of contractor operations. mark was named vice president in 1997, promoted to senior vice president in 2004 and executive vice president of contractor operations in 2015. mark received a bachelor of arts degree in accounting from the university of cincinnati. thad reinhard president of roto-rooter corporation, director of plumbing thad reinhard serves as president of roto-rooter corporation and director of plumbing for roto-rooter services company. thad oversees roto-rooter's manufacturing plant in west des moines, iowa and its production of roto-rooter sewer and drain cleaning machines, cables, blades, and other equipment. he also oversees roto-rooter's network of more than 500 independent franchises in the united states, canada, and around the world. before joining roto-rooter as director of plumbing in september 2017, thad held several positions at ferguson enterprises, the nation's largest distributor of plumbing supplies. he was named president of roto-rooter corporation in december 2017. thad earned bachelor of
