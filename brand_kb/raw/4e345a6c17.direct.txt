the indiana state fair? the 2026 indiana state fair will take place from friday, august 7 to sunday, august 23. this year, the indiana state fair is partnering with the indianapolis indians to celebrate the theme "always a hit." the 2026 indiana state fair runs for 17 days, but is closed on mondays. that means there are a total of 15 days of fun at the fair! come for your favorite fair food, see the animals, ride the rides and take in the sounds of summer at the midway! history of the indiana state fair in 1851, the indiana general assembly passed a law that was, "an act for the encouragement of agriculture." this law created the state board of agriculture whose primary goal was to host the first indiana state fair. the first fair took place in 1852 at camp sullivan, now known as military park. indiana was the sixth state in the nation to hold an annual state agricultural exposition. the indiana state fair was also held in lafayette, madison, new albany, fort wayne and terre haute before it was centrally located to indianapolis in the area that is now the herron-morton place neighborhood. by the late 1800s, the fair had outgrown its location and moved to its current location on 38th street. the indiana state fairgrounds & event center opened in 1892 and has hosted more than 115 indiana state fairs. the indiana state fair has been held every year since 1852, except 1861 because the indiana state fairgrounds were being used for military purposes during the civil war, and in 2020 when it was canceled due to the covid-19 pandemic. during world war ii, the fairgrounds were again requisitioned for the military and were under army air forces use, therefore the 1942-1944 fairs were scaled back to 4-h competitions only. top tips for attending the indiana state fair it is august in indiana; remember to bring sunscreen and water. check out the livestock shows at the indiana farm bureau fall creek pavilion ! the fair hosts a variety of free concerts at the hoosier lottery free stage . from three dog night to lonestar to babyface and the beatles tribute band 1964 the tribute, there's a concert for you. don't forget to see the beloved attractions like the giant cheese sculpture. if you're looking for a break from the heat, be sure to enjoy the state fair splash pad, the newest permanent fixture in meijer family fun park, open daily from 9 a.m. to 8 p.m. meet and pet the baby goats at goat mountain every day from 9 a.m. to 8 p.m. head to the big top circus located in the big top zone & the backyard. shows will take place daily at 1 p.m., 4 p.m. and 7 p.m. check out a full list of free activities at the indiana state fair . and finally, the fairgrounds is 250 acres of non-stop fun-be sure to wear comfortable shoes. head to the
