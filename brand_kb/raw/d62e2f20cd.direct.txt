(image courtesy championship off-road racing) championship off-road racing (corr) officials welcomed amsoil as the series' new presenting sponsor, beginning with the 2026-27 season. the series will be known as corr presented by amsoil for the new campaign, which will once again feature some of the top names in the sport doing battle on top tracks across the southwestern united states, event officials stated in a press release. during the 2025-26 season, corr added new classes, prominent new racers, and a racer network television package that included more than 90 airings across 10 rounds, setting a strong foundation for the coming year, noted the release. corr presented by amsoil is set to build on that foundation in 2026-27 with new venues and additional developments to come. the partnership will provide amsoil with a major presence throughout the corr season, including at-track branding, digital and broadcast integration, and engagement with racers and fans across the series. "this is a huge moment in the history of corr, and we couldn't be more excited to enter this next chapter with amsoil," said corr co-owner jason ramsay. "amsoil has been a major supporter of off-road racing for decades, especially short course racing, and their product is already in many of the vehicles on our starting line. having their name on the series means a tremendous amount to us, and we're excited about what we can build together." "amsoil has a long history of supporting short-course off-road, and we're eager to be part of the action in the southwest. corr is creating new opportunities for racers of all ages and experience levels in a family-friendly, competitive environment. that's important for the future of off-road motorsports, so we're excited to support their efforts," said sara oettinger, amsoil marketing manager. additional announcements about the 2026-27 corr presented by amsoil season will be made in the coming weeks. about championship off-road racing championship off-road racing is a short-course off-road racing series featuring professional, amateur and youth competitors at venues across arizona, nevada and california. corr says that it is committed to delivering competitive racing, creating opportunities for racers at every level and growing the sport of short-course off-road racing for competitors, partners and fans. topics amsoil motorsports off-road & 4x4 sponsorship the shop staff send an email august 25, 2026 1 minute read facebook x linkedin share via email print the shop staff related articles valvoline global is an official supporter of the fifa women's world cup brazil 2027 october 21, 2026 tuff country co-founder kelly davis retires after 38 years in suspension october 16, 2026 combat off road jeep accessories joins mopar affiliated accessories program october 15, 2026 rhino usa secures $15m credit facility to fuel national expansion october 15, 2026 join the shop!
