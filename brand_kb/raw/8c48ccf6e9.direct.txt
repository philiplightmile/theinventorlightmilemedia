"we evaluated the opportunity carefully, and the strategic fit is clear. giant eagle expands our reach into attractive adjacent markets, allowing us to do what we do best: run outstanding stores, deliver fresh foods and convenient meal solutions at affordable prices, and take care of our customers and associates every single day." >>> will giant eagle keep its name after sale to kroger? what will happen to its perks loyalty program? kroger said it plans to build on giant eagle's long history of community engagement by bringing its zero hunger | zero waste impact plan to new communities. "today's announcement marks an exciting next chapter for our team members, customers, vendors and community partners," said bill artman, chief executive officer at giant eagle. "together with kroger, we will be well-positioned to advance our strategy and deliver better quality and service, better everyday value, and a better shopping experience for our customers, while providing greater growth opportunities for our dedicated team members." bill artman, the ceo of giant eagle, said giant eagle will keep its name and continue to run their giant eagle supermarket, giant eagle pharmacy, and market district brands as a division of kroger. artman said kroger also plans to keep giant eagle's myperks loyalty program "while exploring additional opportunities to expand its reach." giant eagle headquarters is expected to remain in cranberry township and operate under the same leadership, according to artman. the release from kroger stated that kroger and giant eagle expect to make "limited giant eagle store divestitures" to complete the transaction. it's unknown how many stores will be sold off. the transaction is expected to close in 2027. last year, giant eagle sold its getgo business to alimentation couche-tard inc. this is a breaking story, and we will be working all day to answer your questions. check back for updates here and on channel 11 news.
