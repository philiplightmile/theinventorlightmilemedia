for national night out 2026 " on facebook linkedin icon share "adt helps bring communities together for national night out 2026 " on linkedin adt is proud to sponsor national night out 2026, which kicks off in communities across the united states on aug. 4. adt employees will take part in hundreds of local events nationwide, joining neighbors and first responders in celebrating community. through adt safe places , adt's corporate giving program, the company will also donate $75,000 across select markets in support of first responders. this marks the fourth consecutive year that adt has sponsored national night out. the annual event brings neighbors and law enforcement together at thousands of local events to strengthen relationships in a welcoming, family-friendly setting. at select events, adt team members will be on hand to help families and small business owners learn more about solutions that can support their security needs. "communities are stronger when neighbors and first responders come together, and that's why we're proud to once again support national night out," said kim miller, chief marketing & communications officer at adt.
