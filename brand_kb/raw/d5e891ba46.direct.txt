can-am, an iconic brand of brp inc., announced its new 2025 utility demo experience tour. for the first time, can-am is hitting the road with a demo tour focused exclusively on its hardworking defender and outlander models. this 10-stop tour invites riders around north america to put the vehicles to the test on the trail and in real-world work environments. each stop of the tour will be led by can-am product experts, offering attendees in-depth insight into the industry-leading features and unique benefits of each model. throughout the 45-minute demo ride, participants will put the vehicles through their paces, experiencing the performance, durability and versatility of the defender and outlander models firsthand. the events will also feature games, prizes, food and exclusive event-only rebates of up to $1000 on select side-by-sides and all-terrain vehicles. to find a stop near you and reserve your spot, visit the can-am utility demo experience site at https://canamutilitydemoexperience.limelightplatformevents.com/ . in addition to the utility demo experience tour, can-am will also be present at several major industry events, including agriculture expos and the iconic professional bull riders (pbr) world finals. five of the six events will include the all-new can-am utility demo track, where attendees can test the defender and outlander models in real-world conditions. 2025 can-am utility event schedule pbr utb world finals -
