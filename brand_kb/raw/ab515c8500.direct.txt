"we look forward to engaging with tsr and building on the success we have experienced through mahindra's partnership with tony and stewart-haas racing. with tony, we have found a partner that shares our core values, which creates an energy that resonates throughout our business. we have seen a positive impact on brand awareness from our involvement in nascar. our dealers get involved with the partnership at a grassroots level with in-store promotions and events; they share stories of customers asking for the tractor that tony uses," said viren popli, president and ceo of mahindra ag north america. "employee engagement around our racing partnerships is also high. we enjoy cheering on our athletes and bonding over post-race analysis. with tony, we have found a partner that shares our core values, which creates an energy that resonates throughout our business." mahindra ag north america, headquartered in houston, is a key player in mahindra group's automotive and farm sector, a world's leading farm tractor company by volume. with a newly introduced lineup of sub-compact and compact tractors, mahindra aims to cater to the diverse needs of its customers, offering equipment engineered for ease of operation and durability. stewart's association with mahindra traces back to 2022, marked by a successful partnership within nascar under stewart-haas racing. he expressed pride in mahindra's decision to extend their collaboration into the nhra. "we're proud to have mahindra join our race team. we've built a successful partnership with them in nascar, and i'm honored they want to join us in the nhra," said team owner tony stewart. "mahindra is a company that believes in accepting no limits. they put people first and believe in working hard, which aligns with the beliefs of our race team. i couldn't be more pleased to represent them." stewart recently joined mahindra in new orleans for their mahindra excelerate national dealer meeting, where they launched the new oja-powered sub-compact and compact line of tractors. this new range of models is mahindra's most ambitious global tractor program to date. running a 414-acre ranch in columbus, indiana, stewart is a proud mahindra owner. "i love getting outside and working on the land when we're not at a race track," stewart said. "it was awesome getting to see the technology in mahindra's new oja-powered tractors in new orleans. i'm looking forward to trying one out at home. we have a rewarding partnership, and i'm proud to be a mahindra customer." bobby bennett thank you for joining us on this journey. your support and trust inspire us every day to deliver the best in drag racing journalism. we are excited about the future and look forward to continuing to serve you with the same dedication and passion that has defined competitionplus.com from the very beginning. all posts share the insights? click here to share the article. click here facebook twitter linkedin ad space x ad space ad space x ad space competition plus team since our inception, we have been passionately dedicated to delivering the most accurate, timely, and compelling content in the world of drag racing. our readers depend on us for the latest news, in-depth features, expert analysis, and exclusive interviews that connect you to the sport's pulse. sign up for our newsletters and email list. x/twitter this field is for validation purposes and should be left unchanged. name first last email breaking news mahindra ag partnering with tony stewart's top fuel dragster in 2024 bobby bennett february 28, 2024 john doe lorem ipsum dolor sit amet consectetur adipiscing elit dolor more posts send us a message full name phone email send bobby bennett thank you for joining us on this journey. your support and trust inspire us every day to deliver the best in drag racing journalism. we are excited about the future and look forward to continuing to serve you with the same dedication and passion that has defined competitionplus.com from the very beginning. don't miss these other exciting stories!
