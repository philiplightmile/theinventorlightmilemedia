mack trucks named official partner of pbr mack trucks named official partner of pbr greensboro, nc (aug. 20, 2026) - mack trucks and pbr (professional bull riders), part of tko group holdings, today announced a multi-year partnership naming mack trucks as official hauler of pbr, making this the first western sport partnership in mack trucks history. attendees of the pbr teams gambler days aug. 21-23 at the moody center in austin, texas, will have a chance to experience a full-branded mack presence. beginning with the 2026 pbr teams season, two mack pioneer™ models will haul the steel infrastructure that forms the backbone of every pbr event - the bucking chutes, panels, gates and posts that encircle the arena. that steel, collectively weighing nearly 75,000 pounds, stretches 1,800 feet when assembled or the equivalent of six football fields of connected steel. it must travel reliably from city to city across the pbr's top u.s. tours. the two mack pioneer models selected for the application are each spec'd with the mack® mp®13 515-horsepower engine and mack mdrive™ 12-speed automated manual transmission. the trucks are spec'd to operate at up to 80,000 pounds gross combination weight (gcw). the 76-inch mid-roof sleeper configuration features a reclining bunk with premium mattress, providing drivers with the comfort needed to keep pace with pbr's demanding travel schedule. as part of the partnership, mack trucks will activate across pbr teams, a 10-team league launched in 2022, and pbr unleash the beast, the sport's premier individual competition tour, bringing its brand directly to fans through a robust mix of physical, in-venue and digital assets. the program includes two co-branded, wrapped mack pioneers deployed through the mack trucks dealership activation program, supported by co-branded marketing materials and rider signings, as well as dedicated concourse and fan zone activation space. mack trucks will also receive in-arena visibility through digital led and second-level signage, along with inclusion across pbr social media, newsletter and website channels. "pbr is built on toughness, and so is mack," said josh baker, chief revenue officer, pbr. "having a truck that can handle nearly 38 tons of steel night after night, city after city, is critical to putting on the best show for our fans coast to coast. mack delivers that." "mack partnered with pbr because of our shared brand values and the opportunity to share experiences with our customers that strengthen our relationships with them," said david galbraith, mack trucks vice president of global brand and marketing. "partnering with pbr gives us a great stage to show what the mack pioneer can do and what the mack brand is all about. we're looking forward to proving that across every stop of the season." the mack pioneer, which began production in 2025 at lehigh valley operations, macungie, pennsylvania, is mack's most aerodynamic and fuel-efficient highway truck to date, delivering up to 11 percent improved fuel efficiency compared with previous models. for more information about mack trucks and the mack pioneer, visit your local mack dealer or www.macktrucks.com. caption: mack trucks and pbr (professional bull riders), part of tko group holdings, today announced a multi-year partnership naming mack trucks as official hauler of pbr, making this the first western sport partnership in mack trucks history. attendees of the pbr teams gambler days aug. 21-23 at the moody center in austin, texas, will have a chance to experience a full-branded mack presence. note to editors: these photos can be viewed and downloaded at https://press.macktrucks.com https://main--vg-macktrucks-com--hlxsites.hlx.page/mack-news/fragments/closing-paragraphs style header with mark
