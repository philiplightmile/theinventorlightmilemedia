ohio farm bureau unveils new exhibit at 2026 ohio state fair by madison macarthur ohio published 2:45 pm et jul. 14, 2026 published july 14, 2026 @2:45 pm share ohio - the 2026 ohio state fair is around the corner, bringing fair activities, food and agriculture into the spotlight. this year the ohio farm bureau will open the new imagine ag exhibit in the ohio agriculture center, presented by heritage partner, nationwide, at the ohio expo center and state fairgrounds. the 75,000-square-foot exhibit represents an investment in agriculture consumer outreach. "the ohio state fair remains our most visible and valuable opportunity to elevate the story of ohio agriculture. through imagine ag, we will share this story with thousands of consumers and take our ag literacy efforts to the next level," said adam sharp, ohio farm bureau executive vice president. "our hope is that the exhibit becomes every family's new tradition and creates a space for investment in the next generation, where lawmakers can see the reality of farming and that everyone walks away with a deeper understanding of food and agriculture." when walking through the exhibit, visitors will see how agriculture has evolved and gain a hands-on understanding of it. they will also be able to speak with ohio farmers and get insights into the issues they face, from weather to technology to animal care and crop management. "this year's state fair will unveil the culmination of an unprecedented $400 million investment supported by the ohio general assembly and gov. mike dewine," the ohio farm bureau said in a news release . "farm bureau and other ohio agriculture groups led the effort for this historic investment that will benefit future generations of 4-h, ffa and other youth participants." within the center, visitors will also find the taste of ohio cafe, featuring food prepared by various commodity groups; the ohio department of agriculture's ohio proud shop and kitchen; ohio veterinary medical association's life and learning center; plus horticulture exhibits and competitions. visitors can partake in storied seasons, which will take them through the lives of ohio farm families in an immersive sensory experience. there is also crop invaders, showing visitors through a game how farmers use precision technology to protect their crops against weeds and insects, while harvest hero will showcase the size, scale and scope of ohio's crop harvest. weather wonder lab shows how weather affects ohio farms from below ground to the clouds above, while creature comforts trail brings visitors to experience the comfort of common animal care as they move toward the ohio veterinary medical association (ovma) animal life & learning center. the buckeye state agquest features interactive kiosks featuring ohio's food and agriculture facts broken down county by county. visitors can also see the story of agriculture through various ages at seasons of innovation. spectrum news contact about rss faq sitemap careers advertise with us certifications terms your privacy rights california consumer privacy rights california consumer limit the use of my sensitive personal information do not sell or share my personal information/opt-out of targeted advertising spectrum news app © 2024, charter communications, all rights reserved.
