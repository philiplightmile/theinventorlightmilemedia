partner for the 2025 season may 13, 2025 polaris and amsoil championship off-road today announce their official partnership for the 2025 season, this partnership highlights polaris' commitment to innovation and growth across the off-road industry. polaris remains focused on offering the most comprehensive lineup of off-road vehicles on the market, improving the riding experience for both utility and recreation. the rzr platform showcases the unmatched power and performance of sport side-by-sides through bold design and class-leading technology. through the rzr racing team, polaris puts vehicles to the ultimate test in the most extreme environments, driving innovation for the future. alex scheuerell, director of polaris rzr racing, says, "polaris is thrilled to expand our long-standing involvement in short-course racing with champ off road. for over 70 years, polaris has designed and tested vehicles to redefine what is possible in an off-road vehicle. from the deserts of dakar and baja, to short courses across the u.s., racing is in our dna - bringing performance innovations from the track to our customers on the showroom floor. polaris is proud to support racers of all levels as we continue to lead the industry as the american powersports company." in 2024, polaris expanded the availability of the race-proven rzr lineup through the launch of two industry firsts: the rzr pro r factory and polaris factory race parts. designed and built in house by the polaris rzr engineering team, the rzr pro r factory is the only oem-produced, race-ready sxs. for riders who demand the ultimate in performance, rzr factory race parts are designed and tested by the rzr racing team, now available for consumers. for more information, visit the official rzr racing page . for those interested in getting more information on the rzr racing contingency program, rzr factory race parts, or to submit an application for rzr racing support, visit the official rzr racing resources page here . now it's time to give a shout-out to the polaris class championship winners from the 2024 season! cj greaves took the top podium spot for the pro sxs / pro stock sxs class. david gay finished as the champ for the pro am sxs class. raymond deininger finished first in the 200 sxs class. ashton dreher won the 170 sxs class championship. a bout polaris: in 1954, polaris unveiled the world's first snowmobile prototype and has since become a leading manufacturer of consumer and professional motorsports vehicles, capable of performing off-road, on-road, and marine tasks. polaris racing is also one of the four big factory racing teams competing in the amsoil championship snocross series. #amsoil championship off-road #polaris off-road #polaris off-road racing #polaris racing
