"austin has been a wonderful promoter of the cornwell tools brand this nhra season, helping our team drive franchise engagement and brand recognition. we cannot wait to continue our support of austin, keeping his positive attitude, fun spirit and winning momentum going strong for the next three years." john force racing quote "everybody at john force racing is excited to grow our partnership with cornwell tools and build upon the mutual successes we have enjoyed together. cornwell has the same values we do at jfr: work as a team and have pride in your product," said robert hight, president of john force racing. "it is so exciting to see how cornwell and all the dealers support jfr! we all win as one big team! if all goes to plan, we will be celebrating championship number 23 together very soon!" about cornwell quality tools: headquartered in wadsworth, ohio, cornwell quality tools is the oldest mobile tool company with a market presence since 1919. family-run and employee- owned, cornwell is committed to the pride that accompanies the sale of quality materials. with the use of high-grade alloy steel, combined with modern heat-treating methods, cornwell has continued to produce the finest tools in the world, that have truly been "the choice of professionals®" for 105 years. products are sold via 800 mobile tool dealers across the country. cornwell has two state-of-the-art distribution centers located in clearfield, utah and wadsworth, ohio, along with three manufacturing facilities located in albion, pennsylvania (forging operation), mogadore, ohio (hand tool manufacturing), and van wert, ohio (toolbox manufacturing). for more information, visit www.cornwelltools.com. prev previous next next share: more posts dallas highlights semifinal fallnationals finish for vandergriff peak squad takes no. 1 spot at the metroplex peak squad back in the saddle at the motorplex stay connected race schedule standings facebook instagram twitter youtube contact us home history facilities jfr 101 home history facilities jfr 101 news partners videos race schedule standings nitro mall vintage news partners videos race schedule standings nitro mall vintage © 2026 launch kits made with ❤ by launch kits | columbia, pa
