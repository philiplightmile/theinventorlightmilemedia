"125 years of the chesapeake way" centers on a 1:45-minute short showing new employees being taken on a duck boat tour across several of the bank's branch locations. titled "all onboard," the short was produced by studio bad rainbow and directed by new york-based nick spooner. the campaign also included trimmed-down versions of the video for tv, as well as print, radio, ott, digital and paid social content. local longtime creative nick kaplan launched creative agency another thing with boston-based creatives chris pape and mike cassell. since its launch, the agency has created campaigns for brands like planet fitness , mike's hot honey and shake shack . another thing's work for planet fitness included "rebound & recover," a campaign involving a bus parked outside arenas during march madness that invited fans onboard to test out the brand's spa equipment. the agency's shake shack campaign created limited-edition, disposable shirts made to look like the burger franchise's tray liners as it relaunched its limited time barbecue menu. west cary group was selected as abnb federal credit union 's agency of record. the firm will do media planning, campaign development, tool kit development, performance marketing and advanced regional business analytics for the credit union. west cary group was also awarded a two-year contract with virginia community college system . work for
