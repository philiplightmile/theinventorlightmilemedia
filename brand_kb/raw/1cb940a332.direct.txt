"country music and wrangler have been seamlessly intertwined for decades; it's a fundamental part of our dna," said holly wheeler, vice president of global marketing at kontoor brands. "this summer, we are thrilled to show up for the fans in a big, meaningful way. from the bustling streets of nashville to the sweeping landscapes of montana, we're showcasing the wrangler lifestyle, honoring our heritage, and celebrating the music that soundtracks our brand." the brand's country music celebration officially kicked off this past april in the california desert. embracing the region's massive spring music culture, wrangler made its mark with dedicated off-site consumer events and brand experiences in and around palm springs and indio, setting a high-energy tone for a denim-clad summer. building on that momentum, wrangler's festival tour continued with a massive presence at cma fest in nashville, tennessee, where the brand made its highly anticipated return as an official festival sponsor. wrangler proudly sponsored the remix stage , bringing a high-energy lineup and an unforgettable musical experience to festival-goers. additionally, wrangler continued its long-standing relationship with downtown nashville staple acme feed & seed on lower broadway with a large-scale consumer activation, the wrangler ranch. fans visiting the wrangler ranch experienced the spirit of music city through exclusive artist meet & greets, immersive photo opportunities and games & giveaways. beyond nashville, wrangler's festival footprint will span the country with key activations at: braves country fest (atlanta, ga): merging the love of baseball and country music, wrangler was on-site on june 13th to connect with fans in the heart of the south as their brand endorsee cody johnson headlined the fest. bulls, bands, and barrels (7-stop tour): wrangler will hit the road for all seven stops of this high-energy tour, perfectly blending the adrenaline of western sports and rodeo with premier country music performances. under the big sky (whitefish, mt): set against the breathtaking backdrop of big sky country, wrangler will bring its famed wrangler ranch to a festival that celebrates americana, outlaw country, and roots music. at each stop, wrangler will provide fans with unique opportunities to engage with the brand, celebrate their favorite artists, and experience the timeless durability and style of wrangler denim. for more information about wrangler's summer festival schedule, exclusive merchandise, and ongoing country music sponsorships, visit wrangler.com or follow @wrangler on instagram. about wrangler® wrangler® , of kontoor brands (nyse: ktb), has been an icon in authentic american style for 75 years. with a rich legacy rooted in the western lifestyle, wrangler is committed to offering superior quality and timeless design. its collections for men, women, and children look and feel great, inspiring all those who wear them to be strong and ready for everyday life. wrangler is available in retail stores worldwide, including flagship stores in fort worth and greensboro, department stores, mass-market retailers, specialty shops,
