the title sponsor for the snap-on indycar weekend at the milwaukee mile at wisconsin state fair park, according to an announcement this week. "bringing indycar racing back to the legendary milwaukee mile in 2024 was a win for wisconsin and a win for race fans everywhere," said shari black , ceo of wisconsin state fair park. "we're extremely grateful for snap-on's continued support and its long-term commitment to this event. we look forward to working together to deliver the fastest racing on earth at the world's most historic track." snap-on also has renewed its multiyear partnership with indycar and the indianapolis motor speedway (ims), continuing as the official tools and equipment partner of both organizations. - advertisement - founded in milwaukee in 1920, snap-on operates 15 manufacturing facilities across the united states, including two in southeastern wisconsin. nicholas pinchuk "snap-on has been dedicated to celebrating the pride and dignity of makers and fixers performing critical work for over a century, and milwaukee is a wellspring of the energy that has driven our enterprise from the very beginning," said nick pinchuk , snap-on chairman and chief executive officer. "extending our partnership with the milwaukee mile, indycar, and the indianapolis motor speedway reflects our deep respect for the skilled professionals of motorsports who make spectacular race days possible." the 2026 snap-on indycar weekend at the milwaukee mile takes place this weekend with the snap-on makers and fixers 250 race on saturday and the snap-on milwaukee mile 250 race on sunday. sponsored content could your employees be the next... how the technical college's role is... the deal is done. so what... 100 years of serving wisconsin. building... a practical starting point to explore... bizpeople christine brumfield dylan glindinning chad knutson biztimes milwaukee business news southeast wisconsin\'s source for business news up-to-the-minute business news, thoughtful analysis and valuable strategic insights for milwaukee and southeastern wisconsin business leaders. facebook twitter linkedin youtube google google copyright © 2026 biztimes media. all rights reserved. terms privacy notice at collection your privacy choices terms privacy notice at collection your privacy choices get the free biztimes email newsletter stay up-to-date on the people, companies and issues that impact business in milwaukee and southeast wisconsin sign up now bookmarks for subscribers only bookmarking is only available for paid biztimes subscribers. please log in or subscribe. subscribe now
