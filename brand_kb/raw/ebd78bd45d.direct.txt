tym shines at the 2025 national farm machinery show with innovation, power, and performance back to all news and events tym shines at the 2025 national farm machinery show with innovation, power, and performance exhibition march 7, 2025 tym tractors made a powerful impression at the 2025 national farm machinery show (nfms) in louisville, kentucky, showcasing our tractors and demonstrating our commitment to providing high-performance solutions for farmers. with an extensive lineup of advanced models and technology-driven enhancements, our tractors drew significant attention from those eager to experience the next generation of agricultural machinery. tym's diverse lineup takes center stage from february 11-14, 2025, we displayed our latest innovations and cutting-edge technology to a diverse audience at the kentucky exposition center. from compact solutions to heavy-duty workhorses, our booth at nfms featured models ranging from 20 to 130 horsepower, highlighting efficiency, durability, and performance. key tym models and long-time favorites on display at the show included sub-compact tractors like the t25 , known for their versatility on small farms and property maintenance, offering efficiency without compromising on power. meanwhile, the t115 and t130, utility tractors built for heavy agricultural tasks, showcased our high-performance solutions for demanding farm operations. the latest innovations unveiled: t3025c and t4058p among tym's featured models, two new additions drew significant attention. the t3025c and the t4058p stood out, particularly for their advanced features and comfort-focused designs. the t3025c is equipped with a fully enclosed hvac (heat ventilation & air conditioning) cab, the widest cabin in its class. this feature provides year-round usability, making it an ideal choice for farmers working in diverse climates and conditions. meanwhile, the t4058p, a 58-horsepower tractor, introduced a game-changing feature: integrated telematics technology as a standard inclusion. this advancement allows users to monitor and manage their equipment remotely, offering a new level of control and efficiency. by combining power with the latest technology, tym continues to create reliable, high-performance tractors that meet the evolving needs of today's farmers. in addition to our lineup of subcompact to utility tym tractors, we also showcased tym attachments to demonstrate how the right implements can transform farm machinery and equipment into multi-functional solutions. from loaders to mid-mount mowers, our products offer versatile solutions for various agricultural and land management needs. strengthening market presence in north america with the tym northeast campus building on our momentum in north america, tym made a significant move last summer with the opening of the tym northeast campus in bloomsburg, pennsylvania. this new facility enhances dealer support, improves service response times, and streamlines equipment distribution, ensuring that farmers and agricultural businesses in the northeastern u.s. have quicker access to tym's reliable solutions. this expansion has strengthened tym's presence in the northeastern u.s., enabling better service, faster distribution, and ongoing support for the agricultural community. the national farm machinery show 2025 reinforced tym's role as a key player in the industry, demonstrating our dedication to innovation, performance, and customer support. with new product introductions, exciting advancements, and a growing presence in the u.s. market, we are excited to drive the future of farming forward. as we continue to innovate and expand, we remain connected to the agricultural industry and professionals beyond trade shows.
