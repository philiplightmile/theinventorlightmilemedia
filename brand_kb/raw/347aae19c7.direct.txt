home community home corporate programs firstenergy foundation employee volunteerism education we're going the extra mile to support the akron marathon get ready to lace up your running shoes! we're thrilled to announce that the company will continue to serve as the presenting sponsor of the akron marathon until 2027. this partnership reflects our dedication to sustainability, our commitment to being responsible stewards of the environment, and our support of the communities where we live and work. firstenergy has been a key supporter of the akron marathon, half marathon & team relay, with over 100 of our employees participating in the event each year. in addition to our employees' involvement, we also provide financial contributions, volunteers and other resources to ensure the marathon's success. "we are proud to have supported this beloved akron tradition from the very beginning. and we have continued to sponsor this elite race because it celebrates excellence, promotes a healthy people and planet and showcases the beauty, diversity and tenacity of our city," said ohio president torrence hinton . participants and spectators will see our "experience the energy" branding on sponsorship signs and related materials throughout the event. we are also enhancing the firstenergy finisher festival and block party kits. the akron marathon brings more than $6 million to the akron community annually. additionally, our renewed sponsorship will introduce new green initiatives for the 2025 races, such as using hydrapak cups to reduce paper cup waste at fluid stations and installing branded shoe recycling stations. last modified: october 2, 2026 report outage quick links electric companies connect with us report outage report outage online report by text message text "out" to 544487 report by phone 1-888-lightss (1-888-544-4877) quick links my account payment assistance pay bill analyze usage text & email alerts outages safety save energy scams & fraud start, stop or move requests electric companies ohio edison illuminating company toledo edison jersey central power & light mon power met-ed penelec penn power west penn power potomac edison connect with us x face book linked in youtube flickr copyright© 2026 firstenergy corp. all rights reserved firstenergycorp.com contact us careers investors newsroom empower retirees & alumni cookie preferences privacy notice legal statement terms of use
