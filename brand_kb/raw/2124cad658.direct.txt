"kubota's roster of partnerships includes nascar's trackhouse racing, the national cutting horse association, mlb's texas rangers, and the professional bull riders, and ara shares a lot of the same traits: gritty, outdoors, adrenaline-filled, and proudly american," said theresa duncan, associate director for marketing and advertising, kubota tractor corporation. "as a marketing platform ara represents great value and as a community it represents shared values. we couldn't be happier about extending our partnership." in addition to continuing to serve as the official land management equipment of the ara, the brand's signature bright orange tractors, utvs, and a range of compact construction, turf, and property maintenance equipment will be on full display in shelton, washington, april 17-19 as kubota assumes title sponsorship of round three of the 2026 ara national championship. "last year was kubota's first in american rally, and the fit between their brand and our particular form of outdoor automotive adventure was instant and obvious," said preston osborn, ara series manager. "this year, they're refining their program and adding to their commitment, and this special edition of the olympus rally is the perfect opportunity for kubota, their dealers and customers to deepen their engagement with our sport and our community." the 2026 kubota olympus rally, presented by dirtfish, will mark 40 years since the event was first included on the world rally championship (wrc) calendar-it was a wrc round 1986-1988-and the last time the legendary group b rally cars, widely considered the golden era of global rallying, competed anywhere in the world. to honor the occasion, olympus is expanding to a three-day itinerary, featuring 200 high-speed special stage miles. "the olympus rally is a world-class event, and every year is special, but this year will be one for the ages," said steve mcquaid, olympus rally organizing committee chairman. "having kubota onboard will help us take the rally to new heights and properly celebrate a legendary time in our history, when the world came to washington to witness the breathtaking performance of the group b cars and the bravery and skill of some of the world's best drivers and co-drivers who dared take them to their limits on our forest roads." the 2026 ara national championship kicked off with the sno*drift rally in atlanta, michigan, february 6-7. believed to be the only snow-and-ice rally in the world run without studded tires, sno*drift lived up to its reputation for unpredictability and surprise wins. stars including lia block and travis pastrana led, but circumstances conspired against them, leaving privateer mark piatkowski, both a past ara class champion and the 2022 sno*drift victor, in a perfect position to claim the win. piatkowski leads the ara national championship coming into round two, the rally in the 100 acre wood in salem and potosi, missouri, march 13-14. he is trailed by sean donnelly and javier castro, but all points are still provisional due to multiple protests and pending appeals. for more information about the kubota olympus rally presented by dirtfish-including the opportunity to get behind the scenes as an event volunteer-please visit: https://www.americanrallyassociation.org/olympusrally . about ara: the american rally association (ara) is the largest rally organization in the united states and promotes the country's only cohesive, nationwide competition for the sport of stage rally: the ara national championship. american rally is sanctioned by usac and promoted by rally forward llc, which was founded in 2024 by rally legend lance smith and is dedicated to advancing the sport of stage rally in america. smith is the founder and ceo of vermont sportscar. american rally provides a direct link to pure automotive adventure: a top-speed race against the clock on real roads specifically chosen to challenge production-based cars and world-class drivers on diverse surfaces that include snow, sand, gravel, dirt and tarmac. to learn more about the ara and its partners, go to www.americanrallyassociation.org
