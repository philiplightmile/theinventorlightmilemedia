departing mccann to take on the global ceo role at wpp creative, succeeding jon cook in overseeing ... --> --> by laurie wilson october 8, 2026 tyler turnbull is departing mccann to take on the global ceo role at wpp creative, succeeding jon cook in overseeing the holding company's integrated creative offering. cook will retain his titles as global ceo of wpp creative and vml through march of 2026 to assist the transition after announcing plans to pursue a new chapter in his career on oct. 2. eric campbell, currently ceo of vml and wpp creative for north america, will succeed cook as global ceo of vml, while continuing to lead wpp creative for the continent. tyler turnbull/courtesy of wpp turnbull, a canadian executive, was appointed global ceo of omnicom group's mccann agency in december after a series of leadership changes at parent company omnicom in the wake of last fall's acquisition of interpublic group (ipg). he joined mccann after close to five years under the titles of ceo and group ceo of former-ipg agency fcb. turnbull's appointment comes as wpp looks to bring its creative agencies into closer alignment with its media, data, production, commerce and technology capabilities. launched in february as part of wpp's elevate28 strategy, wpp creative brings together agencies including vml, ogilvy, grey, burson and akqa. "tyler is a world-class leader with the ambition, global perspective and creative conviction to lead the next chapter of wpp creative," said the holding company's ceo, cindy rose. "his appointment reflects our determination to bring together the very best of wpp for our clients, put creativity at the centre of growth and set a new standard for the industry. i am delighted that he is joining wpp." turnbull said he aims to connect wpp's resources while preserving the distinct identities and cultures of its creative brands. "my ambition for wpp creative is to set a new standard for our industry," turnbull said.
