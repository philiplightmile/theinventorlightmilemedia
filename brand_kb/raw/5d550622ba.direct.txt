"we took a new approach to this year's heisman campaign, but one thing is unchanged: these spots reflect nissan's passion for going the extra mile and matching the drive of america's favourite athletes," said tiago castro, senior vice president, us marketing & sales, nissan and infiniti. "just like heisman winners on the field, nissan's competitive spirit drives us to win customer hearts with highquality products, outstanding customer experience and engaging communication." for tell them a heisman sent you , a deep bench of fresh and familiar talent brings their competitive drive to a new challenge: endorsing their favourite nissan vehicles and proving who deserves the title of top spokesperson. the roster features travis hunter, derrick henry, tim tebow, eddie george, barry sanders and jim plunkett. this year, the campaign also adds the newest heisman trophy winner and 2026 no.1 overall pick, fernando mendoza, who makes his debut later in the season alongside the all-new 2027 nissan rogue hybrid. nissan - tell them a heisman sent you official trailer credits view on
