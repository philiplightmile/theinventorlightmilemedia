# Second channel plan (parsed from Philip's mission, 2026-10-26)

Goal: at organizations already contacted, find operations, HR/people and organizational performance leaders (the first wave reached safety, L&D and inclusion people). Output is a ranked shortlist for approval. Nothing is sent or drafted. Lusha spend needs approval, with count and estimated cost shown first.

## Roles (priority order)
- Tier A: Chief Operating Officer, SVP or VP of Operations.
- Tier B: Chief People Officer or CHRO, VP of HR or People, VP of Talent and Culture.
- Tier C: organizational effectiveness, organizational development, operational excellence, human and organizational performance leads.
- Tier D (rank last): EHS director, only if the first contact at that org was not already in EHS.
- Cap: two new people per org, at most one per tier, never the same person or the same function already contacted.

## Search budget per org
At most 4 queries: leadership page, "[org] COO" or "[org] chief people officer", "[org] operational excellence" or "[org] organizational effectiveness", one press-release or conference query. Record name, exact title, tenure start, source URL and date, one signal, an evidence quote of 25 words or fewer, and confidence (confirmed = two sources or an official page, likely = one source, unclear).

## Outputs (later)
second_channel.csv, SUMMARY.md, LEARNINGS.md update after every 10 orgs. Stop after 100 orgs, three weak batches in a row, or on Philip's word.
