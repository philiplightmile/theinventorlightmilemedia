# NFL youth programming intel and workshop fit (deep pass, 2026-10-14)

31 teams (all but the Browns), one agent each, instructed to open 8+ pages. Actual coverage varied: most opened 8 to 14 pages but ran only 2 to 10 searches, and many skipped Juneteenth, STEM, entrepreneurship and Inspire Change searches. "Fit" is my read of the agent findings. Dates are as published; "undated" means the page showed none. No emails sought.

## What the evidence shows
- Philip's hunch is mostly right: nearly every team runs year-round youth programming (mentoring, career exploration, school visits, literacy, Boys & Girls Club ties), and many fund urban or underserved youth through NFL Inspire Change grants.
- Dated Black History Month youth programming in 2025 or 2026 is thinner. Opened-page evidence: Bears (Feb 2025, Feb 2026), Bills (Black Carpet Feb 2025 and 2026; Story Hour 2025, 2026), Chargers (Feb 2024, 2025, 2026 outings), Ravens (essay contest 2026), Rams (Feb 2025), Commanders (Feb 20, 2026), Steelers (Living Museum, Feb 12, year inferred 2026; Barrett Elementary Feb 28, 2025).
- Entrepreneurship is the rarest theme: Raiders (Hip-Hop Entrepreneurship Program Academy, 2025 Inspire Change Changemaker), 49ers (Inspire Change Cultural Tour pairing high schoolers with Black entrepreneurs, Dec 2023), Eagles (Entrepreneurs Game Plan with Firstrust, high school), Jaguars (annual "Creating Black History" series profiling Duval Black entrepreneurs, pages undated, general audience).

## Hypothesis: where Philip's workshop fits
1. **Slot into an existing February series** (Bears, Chargers, Bills, Ravens, Rams, Steelers, Commanders, Jaguars). Hook: Morgan's story as the entrepreneurship and innovation counterpart to their existing Black History Month content.
2. **Attach to a career or entrepreneurship event for high schoolers** (Raiders, 49ers, Eagles, Seahawks, Chargers Bolt Academy, Lions Academy, Texans career days). Hook: a Black inventor who turned a good idea into a business against the odds.
3. **Go through a team-funded youth nonprofit** (Inspire Change grantees: Colts CLD, Bucs YET Center, 49ers grantees, Jets partners, Giants partners). Lower approval friction than the team itself.
4. **Propose a Black History Month equivalent of an existing heritage series** (Cardinals, Panthers, Saints each run Latino Heritage Month school visits). Weakest evidence, biggest ask.
5. **School-visit request forms exist at some teams** (Rams STEAM form; Titans Take A Titan To School). A direct route if a team accepts outside partners.

Biggest risk: most programs are sponsor-driven or player-led and no team page says it accepts outside workshops. February 2027 calendars may already be set.
