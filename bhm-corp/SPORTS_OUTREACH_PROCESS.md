# Sports organization outreach: process design (draft, no searches run yet)

Date: 2026-10-14. Origin: Browns thread (Andrew Berry, then Peter John-Baptiste, CCO, and Renee Harvey, VP Community Impact and Foundation). Treat the Browns as a live conversation, separate from this pipeline.

## Decisions from Philip
- Audience: students and youth (the young people each org's programs mentor). Not staff or fans for now.
- Universe: Cleveland teams first, then all 32 NFL teams and their foundations.
- Channel: Harvard alumni platform. Claude drafts text only; Philip sends.
- Delivery: open to travel for in-person February sessions anywhere, virtual as the alternative.

## The play being repeated (hypothesis, n=1)
1. A hook taken from the org's own work (the Browns note cited their Be The Solution video).
2. Student framing: help young Black students see themselves as entrepreneurs and innovators, with the VC funding gap as the stakes.
3. A local tie to Garrett Morgan's story where one exists (Cleveland).
4. A low ask: a February workshop or a Zoom call. No price.
5. A warm referral: the first contact routes us to the community-impact owner.

Unknown: whether the channel (alumni platform to a Harvard-affiliated contact) or the message earned the reply. Track both.

## Steps
1. **Universe list.** Cleveland teams and their foundations, then NFL teams (32) and each team's foundation or community arm. Later: NBA, MLB, NHL, MLS, WNBA.
2. **Signal research (per org, capped searches/pages).** Dated evidence in the last 18 months of: Black History Month programming, youth mentoring or education programs, entrepreneurship or social-justice initiatives. Record URL and date, or "none found". Mark snippets "snippet, not opened". Never infer.
3. **Owner research.** Target community impact, foundation, community relations, and youth-program leads. Not owners, presidents or GMs. Public staff and foundation pages first; Lusha only if needed (about 90% of reveals are compliance-restricted).
4. **Harvard alumni check.** The alumni platform only reaches members of the Harvard alumni community. Claude cannot see the directory. Claude can flag contacts whose public bios mention Harvard; Philip confirms membership in the directory. Contacts who are not reachable this way fall back to a Gmail draft or a warm referral.
5. **Score and tier.** Tier 1: Cleveland tie or strong dated youth programming, with a named owner and a Harvard flag. Tier 2: programming evidence, owner named, no Harvard flag. Tier 3: programming evidence only, no named owner.
6. **Draft.** One per org, text only for the alumni platform. Hook sentence rewritten from the org's own program; rest follows the Browns note. No price, no new claims. Philip reviews before sending.
7. **Track.** Add to `candidates.csv`: signal, signal_url, signal_date, tier, channel (alumni or Gmail), owner role, outcome. Log in `LOG.md`.
8. **Read out.** Compare positive-reply rates by tier and channel. Directional until about 40 delivered per cell.

## Open questions for Philip

- Browns: do you want a separate follow-up plan, and what did they say beyond the 30-minute ask?
