# NFL youth program fit for a Garrett Morgan workshop (2026-10-14)

Research by agents, mostly 2 searches and 2 to 3 opened pages per team. Items marked "snippet" were not opened. "Fit" is my read of the agent findings. Philip asked to skip emails for now.

## Strong
- **Chicago Bears.** Only team with dated Black History Month youth programming: "Love, Legacy and the City" (announced 2026-02-02, includes the Black History Month Youth Leader Award and BAM/WOW school visits). Also the Dream Summit Experience (2026-10-08, ~70 students from mentoring groups). Owner seen: Mallorie Sanders, DEI and social impact programs manager. No STEM or entrepreneurship program named. https://www.chicagobears.com/news/chicago-bears-celebrate-black-history-month-through-storytelling-community-and-legacy-driven-programming
- **Philadelphia Eagles.** Entrepreneurs Game Plan with Firstrust Bank (high schoolers, business simulations, pitch competition; page undated, references a 2026 event) and Tackling STEM with Braskem (middle school, 2024-11-29). Best entrepreneurship fit found. No Black History Month item since April 2025. https://www.philadelphiaeagles.com/fans/firstrust-gameplan/
- **Cleveland Cavaliers (from round 1, not an NFL team).** Cavaliers Social Impact Academy, 20 Cuyahoga High School students (2025-09-29).

## Moderate
- **Indianapolis Colts.** Partnership with the Center for Leadership Development, African American youth grades 4 to 12 (gift dated 2020-07-22; current status unverified; CLD is a partner, not Colts staff). Black History Month items are snippets only.
- **Washington Commanders.** Black History Month event 2026-02-20 at Kimball Elementary with DC Central Kitchen (second annual); Youth Academy Junior Pilot for grades 4 and 5 launched 2026-09-15.
- **Carolina Panthers.** Community Innovation Award with Honeywell (2025 season; $5K grants to youth STEM groups such as FIRST Robotics NC). No Black History Month item since 2021.
- **Tennessee Titans.** ONE Community with Tennessee State University (announced 2022-10-20; scholarships and job shadowing). Program lead named in a 2022 source: Johari Matthews.
- **Pittsburgh Steelers.** Steelers STEM with U.S. Steel, grades 6 to 8 (2021, not verified as current); Jerome Bettis Leadership Award.
- **Detroit Lions.** Detroit Lions Academy Career Exploration Day (2026-10-07).
- **Baltimore Ravens.** Black History Month Essay Contest for middle and high school (opened page shows only 2022).
- **Buffalo Bills.** M&T/Everfi financial literacy program, grades 4 to 6 (undated).
- **New York Giants.** Flag Football Youth Mentorship Program with the NYC Police Foundation (2025-07-23); no STEM or entrepreneurship program found.

## Weak
Cincinnati Bengals, Jacksonville Jaguars, Atlanta Falcons, Houston Texans, New York Jets. No youth STEM, entrepreneurship, or dated Black History Month program found. The Jets search used only 2 of 5 searches, so a follow-up could change that.

## Caveats
- Only the Bears and Commanders have dated Black History Month youth programming since April 2025.
- No team-run entrepreneurship program was found except the Eagles.
- Many findings are snippet-only or undated.
