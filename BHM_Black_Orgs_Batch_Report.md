## 6. Feedback for the Gemini prompt (additions to section 5 of the Final Report)

1. **Do not write "Date Checked" or "Active" unless a page was opened.** Every row carried them, and about three quarters of the pages failed to load.
2. **Give the URL the inbox actually appears on.** Most stated contact URLs were 404. Ask Gemini to paste the printed line containing the email, with the page URL.
3. **Evidence URLs must point to the event page, not /news.** Ask for the event's own URL, host, year and one quote.
4. **No bulk templated inboxes.** Patterns like `info@`, `history@`, `community.relations@` across 120 rows were guesses. Ask for "not found" instead.
5. **State-law column:** require the actual law number and whether the specific institution is covered. Several of this CSV's citations were wrong or vague.
6. **Skip categories that do not book outside speakers.** In this batch, hospitals, banks, and associations produced no drafts. Divine Nine HQs and church denominations produced one. Chambers, film festivals, and HBCU departments produced most.
7. **Add a "named programming contact" column** and require the exact title as printed.
