## Strongest leads, in plain terms
1. **Wahl.** October 2025 release opened: its mobile barbershop gives free cuts at community events with charity donations. Cleveland barber-school scholarships (2021). Best fit for a barbershop series.
2. **Sally Beauty.** 13-stop HBCU campus tour through June 2026 (release dated 2026-02-27), framed as "community," not DEI.
3. **Wolverine.** Jordan Davis Trades Tour (8 prizes of $7,500) and "over $2 million" from Project Bootstrap (2025-05-07). The tour is a national contest, so comparability is moderate.
4. **Goodyear.** Akron home base, 2025 blimp centennial events, safety-and-mobility language. Offset by heavy restructuring.
5. **ADT.** National Night Out $75K (2026-08-03) and Marlins small-business pop-ups, a good fire-department fit. Offset by the 2026 breach.
