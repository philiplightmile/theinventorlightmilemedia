# Donor Prospect Research: Fixed Rules (the agent may NOT edit this file)

Purpose: build an evidence-based list of potential supporters (individuals, family foundations, funds, corporate giving) for *Standing in Fire*. Research and qualify only. Philip reviews and sends everything.

1. Public sources only: donor honor rolls and annual reports, foundation filings (e.g. ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer / 990-PF), org board pages, press, film credits, institutional news. Open the page. Write "opened" or "not opened". Never write "verified".
2. Never guess URLs. Use only URLs from search results or links seen on an opened page.
3. No net worth or income claims, ever. No wealth-screening estimates. Screen on documented giving and affiliations.
4. No personal emails, phone numbers, or home addresses. Contact route = organization, foundation office, or "warm intro needed". Metro-area location only.
5. No political donation data (FEC, state) used for targeting.
6. Record only people with public donor recognition, a public role (board, trustee, officer, foundation principal), or a public statement tied to the cause. Do not record family members or private individuals with no public giving record.
7. Each row needs a dated evidence page opened (within 5 years for giving; any date for stated personal tie). Wealth alone = drop. Own words for evidence summaries, no long quotes.
8. Exclusions: Greater Cleveland Film Commission, Western Reserve Historical Society, Cleveland International Film Festival, City of Cleveland, Cleveland Foundation (declined), anyone in ../excluded_orgs.txt, anything already in prospects.csv.
9. Tiers: A = documented gift to a comparable cause (film, Black history, Cleveland civic/arts, safety/fire service, STEM/invention education). B = public role at a relevant institution. C = personal tie to the story (Kentucky/Paris KY roots, Cleveland native, safety-industry founder). No tier = drop.
10. Do not draft or send anything. Do not open Gmail.
11. Claims about the film come only from ../CLAIMS.md.
12. Commit and push to branch claude/beautiful-wozniak-d53t0b after each iteration (git pull --rebase first). Never force-push.
13. Stop conditions: iteration cap in STATE.md reached; or a safety/privacy doubt about a row (then drop the row).
