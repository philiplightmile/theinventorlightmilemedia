# Final Report: Donor Prospect Research (8 iterations, Oct 8, 2026)

Scope: foundations, funds, corporate giving programs and institutions only (named private individuals excluded by decision). Every row comes from a page I opened. Nothing was drafted or sent.

## Result
- 93 organizations in `prospects.csv`: Ohio 37, Kentucky 23, Los Angeles 21, Segments (national, fire service, film) 12. No New York rows beyond a few segment rows: New York is the weakest territory.
- 34 funders appear on more than one opened Cleveland donor list (`PRIORITY_OVERLAP.md`).
- `ELIGIBILITY.csv`: 10 funders checked for fiscal-sponsor and film eligibility. Only Gund and Reinberger were checked against official guidelines I opened.
- `DROPPED.csv`: funders removed after opening (Ford JustFilms, Perspective Fund, MacArthur/Tribeca). `leads_to_open.csv`: leads I could not open.
- `INTRO_MAP.md`: institution-level notes for asking your own network for introductions.

## What the evidence says
Strongest evidence of comparable giving: funders on several opened Cleveland lists (Fowler, Kulas, Murch, Laub, Jennings, O'Neill Brothers, Lincoln Electric, KeyBank, PNC, Lakeside), the Karamu House funders (Reinberger, Shubert, Codrington, Murphy, Treu Mart, McGregor), film-industry funders of the Academy Museum (Hobson/Lucas, Roth, Simms/Mann, Wasserman, Ahmanson, The Film Foundation, Netflix, Participant, J.P. Morgan, Campari), Kentucky Black history and humanities funders (Mellon, Kentucky Foundation for Women, Kentucky Humanities donors), and fire-service and safety sponsors (3M Scott Fire & Safety, Motorola Solutions Foundation, Pierce, Cintas, UL, FM Global).

## What eligibility checks changed
- Gund: its current guidelines (opened, Aug 18, 2026) do not prioritize film productions. Downgraded.
- Reinberger: official site opened. 501(c)(3) required, fiscal sponsorship not addressed, wants a conversation first. Best-documented live route.
- Murch, Frazier-Joy: invite-only. Not cold-outreach targets.
- Kulas: accepts unsolicited applications per one aggregator; focus (music) and fiscal-sponsor policy unverified.
- Laub: sources conflict on whether it takes applications.
- Martha Holden Jennings (K-12 school partners), Nord (Lorain County): likely poor fits per search summaries.
- Fowler: one source said grantmaking ends Dec 31, 2025; the page I opened did not say so. Verify before contact.

## Unverified or weak
- Overlap on donor lists shows general Cleveland giving, not interest in film or Black history.
- Several source pages are undated (NFFF sponsors, Academy Museum founding supporters, Kentucky Humanities scroll).
- Lakeside Foundation: the name on the Cleveland lists may not be the Pennsylvania entity search found; identity unconfirmed.
- Eligibility for most funders rests on aggregator summaries. Fiscal-sponsor acceptance (Fractured Atlas) is verified for no funder except Gund (accepts via the sponsor).
- No contacts were collected. Almost no funder has a verified application route.

## Process lessons (how the loop self-corrected)
- Iteration 1: PDF donor lists were the best source. Iteration 2: I added rows I had not opened, violating my own rule. Iteration 3: caught it, moved them to leads. Iteration 4: opening leads first lifted the opened-page rate from 22 to about 80 percent. Iteration 5: one experiment failed (site-name prefix in search). Iteration 6: cross-list overlap found a new ranking method. Iteration 7: eligibility checks overturned the overlap ranking for Gund. Metrics are in `METRICS.csv`.
