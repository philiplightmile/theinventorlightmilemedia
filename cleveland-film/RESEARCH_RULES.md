# Cleveland Film: Research Rules (research only)

PURPOSE: find organizations and evidence only, for support of *Standing in Fire* (Garrett A. Morgan feature film, Cleveland). No drafting, sending, or collecting names/emails.

RULES
- Never guess a URL. Use only URLs from search or seen on a page you opened.
- Never write "verified". Write "opened" or "not opened".
- Per organization: open homepage, then one evidence page dated within 18 months (open grant cycle, guidelines, award list, program page) or the current guidelines page.
- Record applicant eligibility exactly as stated (nonprofit required? fiscal sponsor allowed? Cleveland/Cuyahoga/Ohio residency? film projects allowed?). Our fiscal sponsor is Fractured Atlas. If a funder excludes fiscally sponsored projects, drop it. If not stated, write "not stated".
- Record the next deadline or cycle if stated; otherwise "not stated".
- Fit: High = opened evidence shows an open or recurring program matching film/history/Black history/education AND we appear eligible or could be via fiscal sponsor. Medium = mission fit only. Drop everything else. Do not speculate about award amounts.
- Skip anything in excluded_orgs.txt and anything already in another research file.
- First line of the file: counts (considered, dropped by reason, kept).
- About three searches per organization, then move on.

COLUMNS: Organization | Territory | City, State | Program or unit | Evidence page URL | Evidence date and what it shows | Applicant eligibility (as stated) | Next deadline or cycle | Fit | Notes
