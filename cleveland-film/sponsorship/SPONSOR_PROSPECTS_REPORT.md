# Sponsorship Prospect Report: STANDING IN FIRE

Prepared Oct 22, 2026 for Philip Musey, Lightmile Media. Neutral research report. Nothing here states cast or financing as confirmed.

**Labels.** [V] verified: I opened the primary page and read it. [I] inferred from evidence. [U] unverified: seen only in a search summary or secondary source, or not found. Dates are the publication date where one was shown; "undated" means none was shown.

**Method and limits.** I ran roughly 100 searches and opened roughly 30 pages. The search tool returned little 2025 and 2026 coverage for most brands, so most "no evidence found" results mean not found, not absent. No reliable spend figures were found: MediaRadar profile pages (undated) say Unilever, P&G and L'Oreal each spent "over $100 million" in digital, print and national TV, which covers only part of their media and is an estimate. Nothing below relies on those figures.

**Scoring.** Story fit (S), capacity (C), reputation risk (R, 5 = low risk) and access (A) are scored 1 to 5. Demonstrated behavior (D) and timing (T) are scored 1 to 5 and doubled. Total = S + C + R + A + 2D + 2T, out of 40. Capacity and risk are largely inferred.
