# Cleveland Film Support Outreach: Playbook

Adapted from the BHM repeatable outreach playbook. Offer: support (funding, fiscal sponsorship, partnership, in-kind) for the feature film *Standing in Fire* about Garrett A. Morgan, filmed in Cleveland. Not a sale.

## Differences from BHM
- The ask is support, not a purchase. Targets are funders, partners and facilitators, so expect 15 to 30 organizations, not 100. Personalize each.
- Level A "in-market" evidence = an open or upcoming grant cycle, stated eligibility match, or a past award in film/history/Black history. Level B = recurring funding or partnership in this area. Level C = mission fit only (Medium). Level D = drop.
- Check applicant eligibility first. Many funders need a 501(c)(3) or fiscal sponsor. Record it in the research file. Do not draft outreach to a funder we cannot legally apply to until the fiscal-sponsor question is decided (see OPEN_DECISIONS.md).
- Warm contacts (Greater Cleveland Film Commission, Western Reserve Historical Society) are existing relationships. Do not cold-email them. Route through Philip.
- Cleveland Foundation declined on Aug 10, 2026 (strategy fit). Only a brief courtesy update, no re-pitch.

## Pipeline
1. Rules first: RESEARCH_RULES.md, CONTACT_RULES.md, excluded_orgs.txt, CLAIMS.md.
2. Research by territory (research/Tn-<territory>.md, identical columns).
3. Merge, de-duplicate, exclude.
4. Contacts via script, separate rules (reuse ../scripts/discover_contacts.py).
5. Spot-check 10 rows.
6. Check sent Gmail by domain before drafting.
7. Draft in Gmail from TEMPLATES.md. Philip reviews and sends.
8. Log in TRACKER_COLUMNS.md sheet.
9. Follow up once, 7 to 10 business days after send (funders move slower than buyers).
10. Re-measure at 2 weeks and 30 days.

## Territories
T1 Local arts/culture funders (CAC, Assembly for the Arts, Ohio Arts Council)
T2 Local private foundations
T3 State film incentives and film offices (warm: GCFC)
T4 Black-led film and arts orgs; potential fiscal sponsors
T5 History and humanities (Ohio Humanities, museums, historical societies)
T6 Education and safety-industry sponsors (school district, universities, safety associations/companies)
T7 Location and permitting (water authority, city offices)

## Rules that carry over
Opened vs not opened (never "verified"). No guessed URLs. No personal emails. General program inbox over named person. One campaign subject line. Humans send. No price/ask amount in first email unless the funder's process requires it. Every claim backed by CLAIMS.md.
