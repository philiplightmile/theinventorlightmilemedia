# Standing in Fire: Funder Prospect List

Prepared Oct 8, 2026 for the film fundraiser. Companion document: `REPORT_OUT_Donor_Research.md` (how this was built).

**The project:** *Standing in Fire*, a feature film about Garrett A. Morgan and his 1916 rescue at the Lake Erie Waterworks Tunnel, to be filmed in Cleveland. Fiscally sponsored by Fractured Atlas (fundraising page: https://fs.fracturedatlas.org/memberships/a206d6ba-a782-4d04-aa38-c184c6094c89). Goal: raise $500,000, half of a $1,000,000 budget, held in escrow toward an Ohio Motion Picture Tax Credit application. The City of Cleveland (Mayor Justin M. Bibb) issued a letter of support dated Oct 7, 2026. The letter is not a financial commitment and the City has not audited the financing; please describe it as support, not funding.

## How to read this list
- **Scope:** foundations, funds, corporate giving programs, and institutions only. No private individuals are listed (a deliberate choice).
- **Evidence:** every organization below appeared on a page we opened (donor list, sponsor page, grant record, or guidelines). "Fit" means evidence of giving to a comparable cause, not a confirmed willingness to fund this film.
- **Contacts:** we did not collect personal emails or phone numbers. Where a general contact was printed on a page we opened, it is listed. Otherwise the route is "foundation website or grants office, to be looked up." Items marked *unconfirmed* came from search summaries and need checking.
- **Eligibility** (fiscal sponsor, film) was verified against official guidelines for only two funders (Gund, Reinberger). Everything else needs a check before outreach.
