## What the research showed
- The search tool is fuzzy and returns summaries, so query phrasing like quoted OR-lists is ignored. Queries that name a page type ("present a program", "program proposal", "speaker series 2026") hit pages that print emails. Queries that name an audience ("nurse educator", "instructional coach") did not.
- Library "present a program" pages and small historical-society/church pages reliably print a role inbox. Leadership programs, Rotary/Kiwanis sites, JA chapters and university cultural centers mostly use forms, obfuscated links or staff-only emails.
- Kiwanis club sites (*.site.kiwanis.org), Cleveland Leadership Center (cleveleads.org), APGO and the JA national site returned 403 or failed to load. PDFs (chamber class schedules) could not be read through the fetch tool.
- Programs that publish dated sessions on courage or hard conversations are rare; Leadership Austin (Difficult Talks, Nov 16 2026 and Feb 22 2027) is the clearest match found.
- Several libraries print presenter cost tiers (Woodbridge), so budget exists for outside presenters there. Rotary Ann Arbor bars sales or fundraising pitches; a paid offer could be caught by that wording.
- Ticketed series (Pump House) conflict with a "closed audience, no admission fee" licence term; faith and club groups fit it.
- The "last 18 months" test: several proposal pages are undated (copyright 2026 only), so they are B, not A.
- RESEARCH_RULES.md in the repo root says never collect emails. This round's instructions override that for contacts. The root rules file has not been changed.
