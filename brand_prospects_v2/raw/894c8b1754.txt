more can-am x farm rescue a new three-year partnership providing financial support, equipment and fundraising to help farm and ranch families. can-am x farm rescue are partnering to protect a way of life, strengthen communities and preserve agricultural traditions for future generations. "this partnership gives us additional resources to help more families when they need us most," said shari rogge-fidler, executive director of farm rescue. "every new volunteer, every donated dollar and every piece of equipment ensures another farm or ranch family can keep moving forward during a difficult season." what the partnership involves can-am's commitment to farm rescue includes: • $50,000 per year in direct monetary support for three years • two can-am defender fleet vehicles for regular use in farm rescue field operations • a custom farm rescue wrapped top of the line utility workhorse can-am defender max lone star cab hd11 that will be auctioned to leverage additional funds for farm rescue's mission about farm rescue farm rescue is a nonprofit organization that provides planting, harvesting, livestock feeding and other critical agricultural assistance to farm and ranch families facing major illness, injury, natural disaster or other unexpected hardships, helping them operate during difficult times. auctioning off a can-am defender for a cause one custom can-am defender will be auctioned off at the national farm machinery show in louisville, ky, with all proceeds benefitting farm rescue. the defender auction will be conducted by big iron. bidders can register below . bid on the can-am defender max lone star cab hd11
