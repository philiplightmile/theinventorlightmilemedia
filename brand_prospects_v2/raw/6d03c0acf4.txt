messages, signaling the effects of dehydration. photo: 309 productions at 4 p.m. on the dot, the screens were refreshed to display i.v. o'clock; at the same time, 50 liquid i.v. delivery robots (from kiwibot ) were deployed, giving out reusable water bottles, liquid i.v., and directions to hope hydration stations steps away for clean water. photo: 309 productions produced by advertising agency
