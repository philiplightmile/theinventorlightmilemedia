contact us home news news by topic story aep ohio celebrates the people behind the power in honor of lineworker appreciation day april 14, 2022 • news release aep ohio, an american electric power (nasdaq: aep) company, is celebrating the people behind the power in honor of national lineworker appreciation day on april 18. the day is set aside to give thanks to those who work hard to power communities around the clock. these workers are committed to doing a challenging but necessary job that takes skill and dedication, often requiring long hours away from loved ones. "we're incredibly grateful for our lineworkers who work so hard to provide safe, reliable electricity to help keep our customers and communities connected," said marc reitter, aep ohio president and coo. aep ohio customers are invited to join the celebration and share a message of gratitude by visiting aepohio.com/thankyou. messages will be shared with the company's nearly 650 local lineworkers as part of the celebration. individuals interested in learning more about a career in linework can visit aep.com/careers for additional information. share topic view all topics get alerts get the most recent updates on what's happening at aep. subscribe related news june 18, 2025 feature embracing heritage: 2025 berg juneteenth celebration on june 17, the black employee resource group (berg) celebrated juneteenth with an event hosted at aep's transmission headquarters in new albany, ohio. the event featured a vendor marketplace that allowed guests to sample and purchase items from local black-owned businesses and a performance from mamady mansaré iii of the african cultural arts institute. jan. 10, 2025 feature listen now: conversation with kelly ferneau kelly ferneau, executive vice president and chief nuclear officer, discusses her journey into the nuclear industry and her current role at cook nuclear plant. may 7, 2024 feature aep economic developers teach leaders to support communities economic development is a team sport across our footprint. view more news stories b2b & suppliers community involvement environment recreation safety & health sustainability powering homes and communities for more than 100 years. required internet postings privacy policy cookie settings your privacy choices use of this site constitutes acceptance of the aep terms and conditions. ©1996- 2026 american electric power company, inc. all rights reserved.
