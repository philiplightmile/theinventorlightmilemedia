"our associates are the heartbeat of our company," said owner and chairman alex melvin. "as rural king continues to grow, this new store-support center ensures our teams have the space, resources and technology needed to support our stores and customers while keeping our roots firmly planted in the community we call home." according to the retailer's website, rural king was founded in mattoon in 1960, and the community is home to the chain's corporate offices, distribution center and flagship store. the latter is currently located at cross county mall, about a mile west of the construction site. the chain said that the mall location "will be back-filled once the store has relocated." locally, rural king has a location at 913 w. marketview drive in champaign, just south of sam's club in the north prospect avenue shopping corridor. by the end of the calendar year, rural king will have 162 stores in 18 states and employ nearly 10,000 people, company officials said. this includes over 700 people in coles county. the store-support center will house the company's leadership and support departments, including merchandising, it, finance, operations, marketing, human resources and legal offices, per an announcement from the state of illinois. having the flagship store directly connected to the store-support center will allow team members to test "new ideas, strategies, layouts and operational initiatives," rural king officials said. the event center will be able to hold about 1,500 people, enabling rural king to host company meetings, training sessions and other functions. the venue will also be available for community use, such as for conferences, trade shows and concerts. architectural expressions llp of champaign is leading the project design. state officials said that the project has received support from the state's economic development for a growing economy tax-credit incentive program, with rural king committing in the agreement to a $75 million investment, 100 new full-time positions and the retention of 719 jobs. additionally, the illinois department of transportation is working with the city of mattoon to provide a grant through idot's economic development program to help extend swords drive north from broadway avenue to dewitt avenue to provide access to the new campus. "this investment represents an exciting opportunity for mattoon and coles county and demonstrates rural king's confidence in our community," said mattoon mayor rick hall. "with support from the state of illinois, the city of mattoon, coles county and coles together, this project will help drive future development, create new opportunities and further strengthen our local economy." facebook twitter whatsapp sms email print copy article link save jana wiersema author email latest e-edition already a subscriber? click the image to view the latest e-edition. don't have a subscription? click here to see our subscription options. need help? how to log in navigating the e-edition mobile app download now the news-gazette mobile app brings you the latest local breaking news, updates, and more. read the news-gazette on your mobile device just as it appears in print. newsletter sign up sections home news sports illini sports business arts & entertainment local events announcements obituaries opinion multimedia subscriber services subscription faqs self-service subscription information change of address delivery issues pay bill vacation stop/restart other services advertise with us classifieds employee directory forms library lookup service promote your event search website search archives submit a classified weather where to buy contact information news-gazette.com 2101 fox drive champaign, il 61820 phone: 217-351-5252 email: news@news-gazette.com facebook twitter linkedin instagram ios app android app × browser compatibility your browser is out of date and potentially vulnerable to security risks. we recommend switching to one of the following browsers: microsoft edge google chrome firefox © copyright 2026 the news-gazette 2101 fox drive, champaign, il | terms of use | privacy policy
