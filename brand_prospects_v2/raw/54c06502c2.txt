hockey classic march 28-30, 2025 in rockland, massachusetts march 24, 2025 05:58 pm sponsored by press release nelsonville, ohio - bringing together firefighters, law enforcement and military from across the country and internationally, rocky boots public service division partners with the kingston police hockey classic to raise awareness and funds for first responders. the 11th annual classic will host fifty teams, across eight divisions, to compete for the prize. the event will take place at the lovell ice arena in rockland, mass. "we are really excited to be a part of this year's hockey classic as it supports our first responders who are the backbone of every community. with nearly 800 attendees, this is a special and fun way to thank them for their courage, sacrifice and dedication through a game that we all love," commented mark dean, vice president and general manager of rocky boots commercial military and public service division. the kingston hockey classic tournament is open to all law enforcement, fire, and military teams, and each team is allowed two non-first responders to participate. each team is guaranteed three games, and there will be a playoff round for each division in addition to the championship games on sunday. to top off the festivities a well-known irish band from boston will be performing saturday evening at lovell arena. for more information visit - https://kingstonpolicehockeyclassic.com/ . about rocky founded in 1932, rocky manufactures and markets rugged outdoor, work, and western footwear, apparel, and accessories. born in the small town of nelsonville, ohio, innovation, quality, and durability have always been the rocky heritage, and the brand strives to keep its boots in the backyard with select styles that are now proudly made in the usa with usa & globally sourced components. rocky is a division of rocky brands, a publicly-traded company on nasdaq under the
