president and ceo mcginnis to lead virginia beach subsidiary kate andrews // october 1, 2026 // --> --> listen to this article stihl inc. named caterpillar veteran sean mcginnis its new president and ceo effective immediately thursday, as he takes over responsibility for the power tool manufacturer's virginia beach operations. mcginnis spent more than 25 years at construction and mining equipment giant caterpillar, most recently serving as president of caterpillar paving products, a billion-dollar global subsidiary that makes equipment for road construction and maintenance. he succeeds chris keffer , who left stihl in february as president and ceo and was named ceo of apex tool group in june. lorraine amesbury holder and uwe hirsch, respectively stihl's senior vice presidents of operations and of finance, it, purchasing and quality, shared the president's duties on an interim basis following keffer's departure. "i have admired stihl for years, both as a customer and as someone who understands the value of strong products, dedicated employees and trusted local dealers," mcginnis said in a statement. "i look forward to listening and learning from the stihl team and the stihl dealer network, and working together to build on the company's strong foundation to deliver even greater value to our customers." based in virginia beach, stihl inc. is the u.s. affiliate of the global stihl group headquartered in germany. it employs more than 2,300 people and manufactures more than 100 outdoor power equipment models on its campus in virginia beach. the united states is stihl's largest market, and the u.s. division's products are exported to 80 countries. mcginnis will report to stihl's executive board and will be part of the stihl inc. board of directors and the global executive team, the company said in its thursday announcement. tags: virginia beach , president , stihl , manufacturing ,
