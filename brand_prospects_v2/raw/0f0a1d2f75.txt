shop home news & features team target celebrates the power of community at the 2026 special olympics usa games jul 8, 2026 copy link to share link copied at target, strengthening the communities we serve has long been part of who we are. every june, during community month, we celebrate that commitment through volunteerism, community partnerships and investments that help fuel growth and opportunity for all. this year, one example of that commitment was the 2026 special olympics usa games, where thousands of athletes, coaches, families and fans gathered in minnesota, target's home state. read full transcript fans stroll past a sign that reads 2026 special olympics usa games. emily wears a denim jacket and a red bucket hat with the target bullseye logo. emily: i'm here at the 2026 special olympics usa games here in the tennis center. let's check it out. a sign reads game set love. fans pose for photos by a giant statue of bullseye the dog as a bobblehead mascot. bullseye wears a headband with the target logo. emily shows off her printed photo. emily: we're here at bullseye's tennis club. we have a giant bobblehead of bullseye, and fans can get a gumball with a commemorative pin. fans get plastic eggs from vending machines and check out their prizes. emily strolls through the bleachers. emily: these athletes have come from all over the country. they've worked so hard and i'm so excited to see them here in the twin cities competing for the national title. fans watch tennis matches. a giant tennis ball with the target logo tops another vending machine filled with tennis balls. emily poses for a photo holding an oversized tennis racket. emily: now let's head to fanfest. oh, isn't that what they do in tennis? man: you got it! another team member in a red target bucket hat helps her. team member: welcome, welcome. yeah, this is our giant tennis ball machine. come on, let's grab a spin. he turns a lever, and a tennis ball lands in the slot. emily cools herself off with a pair of cardboard hand fans. emily: just wrapped here at fanfest, which was so much fun. now it's time to head back to the tennis center to meet with april. team members chat by the rows of vending machines. emily and april stand by the statue of the bullseye bobblehead. emily: why is it important for target to show up at events like this? april: so target is not only a sponsor of the 2026 usa games, but we've had countless target team members volunteering, not just here in minnesota, but across the country. sport is such a unifier in general, and this has been such an amazing experience for athletes, coaches, families and fans to come together and really cheer each other on. no matter where you are, no matter who you are, there is a place and a belonging and a role for you to play. athletes hug on the tennis court. fans cheer. a family shows off their commemorative pins. emily: thank you, april, for chatting with us today and sharing your perspective. april: thank you, emily. by the vending machines, april and emily show off their prizes: commemorative pins with tennis rackets and the target bullseye logo. the logo appears over a red background. as a platinum sponsor, official tennis sponsor and sponsor of target village for competitors, target was proud to help welcome athletes from across the country while creating experiences that celebrated perseverance, connection and the joy of competition. the celebration started before the games even began. in communities across the country, tennis athletes visited their local target stores to prepare for the journey ahead, selecting travel essentials, apparel and other items with target gift cards. at target headquarters, team members assembled thousands of welcome bags to greet athletes and coaches as they arrived in minnesota. throughout the competition week, team target helped create memorable moments for athletes and fans alike. from welcoming athletes to target village and celebrating the opening ceremony to hosting interactive experiences at fan fest and recognizing tennis champions during medal ceremonies, our partnership was rooted in one goal: celebrating the extraordinary athletes at the heart of the games. those moments were made possible by the passion of target team members, who volunteered their time, welcomed guests and cheered on competitors throughout the week. together, they helped create an experience that reflected the spirit of the games and the power of community. target at the 2026 special olympics usa games previous slide next slide while the competitions have concluded, the impact of the partnership continues. special olympics is one example of how target brings its commitment to strengthening communities to life-creating opportunities for people to connect, celebrate and support one another. that same commitment extends beyond the games. across our business, we're working to create products, services and shopping experiences that are more accessible and welcoming for millions of guests every day. inclusive design at target we're advancing inclusive design and guest experience across our business. learn more
