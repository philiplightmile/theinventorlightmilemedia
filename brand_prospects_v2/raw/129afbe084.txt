"josh has been an amazing ambassador for our gravely brand," said peter balistrieri, director digital marketing & marketing communications. "we're looking forward to his continued partnership. he's a perfect fit with our values of giving it your all, servant leadership and building enduring relationships." bilicki, who is currently the only driver from wisconsin to compete in the nascar cup series, takes pride in partnering with wisconsin based businesses for his home race weekend. "for the road america race weekend, i'm always very proud to carry wisconsin businesses aboard our racecars," says bilicki. the henry 180 nascar xfinity series race at road america will air live on the usa network on sunday, july 2 at 2:30pm et. about ariensco based in brillion, wisconsin, ariensco is a privately-owned manufacturer of outdoor power equipment for both consumer and commercial markets. established in 1933, the company manufactures equipment under the ariens®, gravely®, rapidcare®, countax®, westwood® and as-motor® brand names. it also established ariensco hospitality with two luxury event venues, stone prairie and round lake farms and is adding the ariens nordic center in 2022. find us on the web: ariensco.com and ariensco.com/rapidcare . join the team at ariensco.com/careers . follow us on twitter , instagram and linkedin . like us on facebook . subscribe to our youtube channel. about gravely® gravely® is a brand extension of ariensco. the brand features a full line of commercial zero-turn lawn mowers available at major dealers nationally. designed for exceptional comfort and built with rock-solid dependability, gravely® has shaped the american landscape for more than 100 years. for more information, visit www.gravely.com. 06.22.22 brands careers history newsroom community out working patents privacy policy website terms & conditions of use terms & conditions of sale contact ariensco © 2026
