wave ® frequently asked questions here's everything you need to know about the worry-free maintenance and premium owner loyalty that comes with owning a jeep ® brand vehicle. when is the jeep wave® program ending? starting model year 2026, jeep ® brand vehicles are no longer eligible for the jeep wave ® program. however, jeep wave still applies to jeep brand vehicles purchased between model year 2021 and 2025. starting with model year 2026 jeep brand vehicles, customers will be able to enroll in the flexcare vehicle protection program-the official extended service contract and maintenance plan for chrysler, dodge, jeep, ram, fiat ® and alfa romeo vehicles. will dealerships honor jeep wave® perks after the program ends? yes, the first two services during the first 24 months of ownership will continue to be honored for eligible vehicles. i was a jeep wave® member before the program ended. can i still use my free oil changes and tire rotations? yes, customers who are already enrolled in jeep wave ® can still use their free oil changes and tire rotations. what happens to the jeep wave® membership if the vehicle is sold? if the vehicle is sold, the jeep wave ® membership for all membership types, along with any unused benefits, is null and void. can members get a refund on the program if they sell their jeep® brand vehicle or it is totaled in an accident? members who purchase a jeep wave ® membership are entitled to a full refund within 60 days of purchase, so long as none of the benefits have been utilized. members seeking a refund should call the dedicated jeep wave member line at 844-jeep-wave (533-7928) for further information. after 60 days, no refund is available. owners who received jeep wave automatically as part of a vehicle purchase are not entitled to a refund what happens to my unused benefits after jeep wave® ends? you will still be able to use your benefits once jeep wave® comes to an end, provided you are still within the 24-month service period. how do i utilize the maintenance services? current jeep wave ® members can go to participating us authorized fca us llc dealers to redeem their oil change and tire rotation services throughout the duration of their contract. what does trip interruption protection cover? the plan will reimburse up to $1,000 per event for lodging, meals and emergency transportation such as taxi, bus or airline for the member and their family if (1) their vehicle is inoperable due to a failure covered under this plan or under the factory warranty and (2) you are more than 100 miles from the address of record. lodging, meals and emergency transportation receipts must accompany a copy of the repair bill and must be mailed to vehicle protection, p.o. box 2700, troy, michigan 48007-2700. there is no maximum number of events that can be covered. what does first day rental allowance cover? first day rental allowance provides a $40.00 ($60 for grand wagoneer and wagoneer) car rental allowance if the vehicle is to be serviced for any same day mechanical repair or maintenance service. vehicles kept overnight are not eligible for first day rental. please note: excludes rental for bodywork to the exterior sheet metal/composite panel or frame collision repairs. the rental vehicle must be obtained from a dealer. if a dealer does not have rental vehicles available, you may obtain one from a licensed rental agency. rental coverage is subject to state and local laws and policies imposed by the rental agency. rental charges in excess of the amount allowed by the plan are the member's responsibility. the plan is not responsible for any refusal of a rental agency to rent a vehicle to you. can i renew my jeep wave® membership before the program ends? no, renewal in the jeep wave ® program has officially ended. how can i check the status of my membership or benefits? current members can go to the mopar ® membership portal to learn more about the current status of their jeep wave
