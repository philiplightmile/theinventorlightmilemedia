shaping the future we firmly believe that our operations must be ecological, economical, and socially sustainable. this attitude shapes the entire group and is reflected in our actions. sustainability management sustainability management we have summarized the focal points of our sustainability management in the "new dimensions" target vision. it describes seven topic areas that set the framework for our activities in the coming years. find out more about our sustainability management products products we take advantage of technological advances and develop innovations that provide solutions to the major challenges of our time. throughout this process, we follow the principles of environmentally compatible product development. find out more about our products supply chain supply chain as a globally operating company, we recognize our corporate responsibility to respect human rights. find out more about our supply chain managements environment environment with its more than 470 locations worldwide, the bosch group has been carbon neutral overall (scopes 1 & 2) since 2020. to achieve carbon neutrality, we employ four levers: improving energy efficiency, generating our own energy from renewable sources (new clean power), purchasing electricity from renewable sources (green electricity), and - as a last resort - offsetting residual co₂ emissions with carbon credits. scopes 1, 2, and 3 are used according to the greenhouse gas protocol corporate accounting and reporting standard. find out more about environmental protection associates associates we want to create an environment that enables all associates to contribute to the company's long-term success because they feel respected at bosch and are able to develop their full potential. find out more about bosch as an employer society society through our actions, we want to promote progress in society and help improve quality of life. find out more about our corporate social responsibility activities facts and figures open and honest, comprehensive and transparent -
