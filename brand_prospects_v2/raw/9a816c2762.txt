"this year was no different, and our booth was jammed as attendees experienced the very latest makita cordless tools and equipment that are replacing products powered by cords, gasoline, and compressed air." the makita booth offered woc attendees the opportunity to experience more solutions for nearly every stage in concrete construction. published on 31 january 2025 share your feedback
