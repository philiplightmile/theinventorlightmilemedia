"john deere and yamaha share a commitment to quality, innovation and strong dealer networks," said jon chase, vice president, john deere turf and compact utility business unit. "this collaboration will enable us to develop differentiated products that excite customers and create meaningful value." "we are excited about this relationship and the opportunities it creates for yamaha, john deere, our dealers and, most importantly, our customers," said dean burnett, president, yamaha motorsports usa. "together, two trusted global brands can accelerate innovation and bring greater value to the market." john deere and yamaha motor will retain their respective brands, dealer and sales channels, and customer relationships. details about future products will be announced when the companies consider it appropriate. for more information on deere & co., visit www.deere.com/en-us/content-library .
