boot to benefit the skilled trades wolverine x metallica scholars launch rancher boot to benefit the skilled trades limited edition collaboration - year five august 1, 2024 "wolverine is proud to continue our partnership with metallica scholars in supporting the next generation of skilled workers. the skilled labor workforce still faces a critical shortage, and this partnership has been pivotal in our team's mission to bridge that gap. we are excited to unveil our fifth collection together, capturing the perfect mix of style, quality, and a great purpose." - scott schoessel, wolverine boots chief marketing officer we're uniting with wolverine boots & apparel once again to champion the next generation of skilled workers. this product launch marks our fifth year partnering with together and to celebrate, we're mixing it up with our first-ever rancher boot. the master craftsmen at wolverine put a heavy metal twist on a classic style. a timeless wellington silhouette allows for easy on and off, while the blacked-out color pattern amps up the edge, topped off with custom skull artwork by artist brandon heart debossed on the upper. whether you're taking on the workday or the snake pit, these badass boots will keep your feet comfortable all day (and night) long. ​ in addition to the limited-edition rancher boots, the wolverine x metallica scholars collection features an assortment of apparel and accessories from previous collaborations, including a trucker hat, t-shirt, bandana, and socks. the best part? 50% of sales from this limited-edition style goes directly to metallica scholars to give more people the opportunity to forge their own paths within the skilled trades. "wolverine has been a strong, dedicated partner to metallica scholars from the very beginning. our team is honored to stand together for the fifth year of our partnership to continue championing the future of skilled workers." - peter delgrosso, awmh executive director shop the collection boot details built to last with premium materials waterproof full-grain leather upper​ breathable waterproof membrane with moisture-wicking mesh lining​ removable, full cushioned footbed for comfort closer to the foot​ full-cushion eva midsole provides stability and cushioning​ slip and oil resistant outsole​ rubber lug outsole grips the ground​ lightweight cement bon-welt construction​ nylon shank provides stability and support shop the collection about wolverine wolverine, the 141-year-old boot and clothing company, is on a mission to honor the spirit and tenacity of the american worker and build the next generation of skilled trades people. taking pride in crafting durable boots with unrivaled craftsmanship and the highest quality materials, wolverine is dedicated to serving hardworking people all over the world. through project bootstrap, wolverine has contributed over $2 million to organizations in support of the skilled trades. for more information, visit www.wolverine.com . wolverine is a division of wolverine world wide, inc.
