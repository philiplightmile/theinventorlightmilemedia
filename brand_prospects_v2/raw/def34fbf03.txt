"within the michelin manufacturing family, the spartanburg plant is a shining example for its safety record," said selleck. "the spartanburg team has implemented programs here that ensure the safest possible manufacturing operation by rewarding safe behaviors. it's evident this plant demonstrates one of michelin's core values - respect for people - every day." in addition to the safety award presentation, the spartanburg facility also reached the milestone of the production of its 50 millionth tire. topics: drivers
