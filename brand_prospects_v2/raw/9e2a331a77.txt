aimexpo floorplan powersports 2026 kawasaki off-road vehicles lineup unveiled explore the 2026 kawasaki off-road vehicles lineup, including brute force, teryx, kfx and mule models. by kyle alexander kyle alexander is the multimedia journalist of motorcycle & powersports news. published: jun 13, 2025 kawasaki has just released its 2026 spring lineup of off-road vehicles atv's and side-by-sides. these include the brute force 750, kfx90, kfx50, teryx krx 1000, teryx krx4 1000, mule pro-dx eps, mule pro-dxt eps and mule pro mx. related articles - kawasaki launches nav 2+2 ptv - segway unveils 'street legal' xyber air electric bike - turn 14 distribution sponsors utv takeover hurricane event brute force 750 in off-road adventures, kawasaki off-road vehicles like the brute force represent reliability, functionality and sporting atv prowess. for 2026, the kawasaki brute force 750 returns as a powerhouse in the atv class with new, striking trims. last year, kawasaki updated the brute force 750 with tft color instrumentation, led lighting and integrated handlebar-mounted led light. kawasaki also added rugged racks in the front and rear to boost durability and utility. with these features, the 2026 brute force 750 stands ready to conquer a wide range of off-road terrain. kfx90, kfx50 for 2026, kawasaki proudly reintroduces the kfx90 and kfx50 atvs for both work and recreational use. kawasaki designed the kfx90 and kfx50 with inspiration from the kx motocross lineup. these models feature an aggressive, dynamic design that sets them apart from competitors. kawasaki built these atvs with features that help young riders enter the world of four-wheel adventure. the kfx lineup offers just the right amount of power and size for new powersports enthusiasts. teryx krx 1000, teryx krx4 1000 for 2026, kawasaki unveils the latest teryx krx 1000 and teryx krx4 1000 sport side-by-sides. these models build on their legacy and offer exciting new options for all types of terrain. kawasaki designed the teryx krx 1000 blackout edition and krx4 1000 blackout edition for nighttime adventures. these editions include all-black interior and exterior finishes, led light bars and a built-in audio system. kawasaki also added sturdy front and rear bumpers and a winch for trail capability and excitement. kawasaki upgraded the standard teryx krx 1000 and krx4 1000 models with showa piggyback shock absorbers. these shocks deliver a smoother ride and enhanced cabin comfort over rugged terrain. kawasaki offers the two-seat teryx krx 1000 in five distinct trim packages. the four-seat teryx krx4 1000 also comes in five different trims, offering variety for trail enthusiasts. mule pro-dx eps, mule pro-dxt eps the diesel-powered mule suits farms, job sites and ranches using a single fuel source. kawasaki built the mule pro-dxt eps diesel with a trans cab system for quick seating configuration changes. users can easily switch between single-row and double-row passenger setups. the 2026 mule pro-dx eps diesel includes a 993 cc diesel engine and tows up to one ton. it also features the largest cargo bed in its class for increased utility. kawasaki adds electronic power steering for easier maneuvering at slower speeds. the chassis features front and rear independent suspension for a smooth ride. the suspension provides 8.7 inches of travel and excellent ground clearance. kawasaki offers four trims in the 2026 mule pro-mx lineup: eps, le, eps camo and se. all versions include electric power steering for greater handling and control. kawasaki designed the mule pro-mx to carry two passengers with power and agility. the vehicle performs well during work and offers fun after the job ends. kawasaki equipped it with a 695 cc single-cylinder engine that produces 42.7 pound-feet of torque. the continuously variable transmission provides smooth and seamless power transfer. kawasaki also includes a selectable two-wheel drive/four-wheel drive system and locking rear differential for more control. mule pro-mx se enhancements the 2026 mule pro-mx se builds on the le edition with several enhancements. kawasaki adds a painted hood, deluxe graphics, cast aluminum wheels and a factory sun top. it also features led inner headlights and electric power steering for better control. a 3,000-pound factory-installed winch with remote control ensures easier towing and recovery. each mule pro side-by-side features a tough chassis and independent front and rear suspension. the suspension gives riders 8.7 inches of travel and great ground clearance. kawasaki built the mule lineup to handle the toughest work demands. every model comes backed by the kawasaki strong 3-year limited warranty. tags: atv , kawasaki , side-by-side , utv print
