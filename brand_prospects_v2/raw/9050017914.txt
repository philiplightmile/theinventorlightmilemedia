back-to-school jul 31, 2025 be prepared, productive and protected this school year with durable, stylish cases for your phone and tablet from otterbox. download: hi res close fort collins, colo. , july 31, 2025 / prnewswire / -- make back-to-school prep a breeze this year with otterbox . with stylish, durable cases for phones and tablets, otterbox keeps your mobile tech protected through every class, commute and late-night cram session. for those looking for extra protection for their tablet, the otterbox symmetry series folio and symmetry series 360 elite offer drop protection that's rigorously designed and tested within a sleek, elegant silhouette. a clear, scratch-resistant back showcases the ipad's design, while the versatile folio front cover safeguards the screen during transit. the interior of the case features a soft microfiber lining to protect the screen, and integrated magnets keep the folio securely closed while activating sleep/wake modes for quick-start functionality and battery preservation. the multi-position stand supports hands-free use for reading, note-taking, viewing, and typing, making it easier to stay productive wherever the day leads. for the ultimate in phone protection for those dashing from class to class, the otterbox defender series pro xt offers seven times the drop protection of military standard. made with a protective dual-layer design and pressure fitted frame that keeps the phone securely in place, defender series pro xt features grippable rubber edges offering both shock absorption and a confident carry. raised bumpers around the camera help shield against hard hits, and port covers block out dust and dirt during everyday use. for those looking to tout a more sophisticated look on campus this year, the otterbox symmetry series cactus leather cases offer an elegant aesthetic in an eco-friendly package. crafted using organically grown and sustainably harvested nopal cactus, the symmetry series cactus leather case combines refined style with trusted protection. the collection delivers three times the drop protection of military standard, with raised edges around the camera and screen for added defense. its ultra-sleek, pocket-friendly design makes it easy to access while built in magnets support wireless charging. for those seeking an artistic touch, the new symmetry series cactus leather embroidery collection adds handcrafted detail to otterbox's signature protection. complete your full back-to-school kit with an otterbox screen protector , mobile charger and magsafe wallet to stay prepared, productive and protected all year long. to view the full line of otterbox phone cases and accessories, please visit otterbox.com . about otterbox: from humble beginnings in a fort collins, colo. garage, otterbox has spent over 25 years mastering the art of protection, delivering durable yet stylish phone cases that can go anywhere, do anything, so you can say 'yes' to everything. whether you're scaling mountains or simply surviving a monday morning commute, otterbox has you and your device covered. it's no surprise otterbox is the #1 most trusted smartphone case brand in the u.s. from ultra rugged to effortlessly stylish, otterbox helps you follow your 'yes' anywhere. otterbox. made for yes . at the heart of every otterbox innovation is a commitment to create positive, lasting change. in partnership with the ottercares foundation, otterbox says yes to giving back by inspiring kids to change the world through entrepreneurship and philanthropy. employees say yes to giving back when they use 24 hours of paid time off each year to volunteer. since 2011, otterbox employees have logged more than 148,000 volunteer hours, contributing an estimated $3.8 million in value to local communities. learn more about our yes at otterbox.com/givingback . for more information, visit otterbox.com . source otterbox for further information: sarah niklas,
