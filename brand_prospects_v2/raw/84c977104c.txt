goodyear closing findlay facility, laying off 85 workers by lydia taylor toledo published 8:13 am et feb. 05, 2026 published 8:13 am est feb. 05, 2026 share toledo, ohio - goodyear tire & rubber company plans to close its tall timbers mold facility in findlay, which will result in 85 layoffs, according to a warn notice submitted to the state. the facility, located at 2025 production drive, is set to close by march 31, according to the notice. separations for employees are expected to begin on or around the 14-day period starting on march 31, goodyear said. the company said it's because of the consolidation of its two u.s. mold facilities into a single location in statesville, north carolina. the akron-based company also announced plans last year to move 175 jobs from akron to costa
