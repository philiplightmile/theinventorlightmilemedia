## Direct conflicts
- Duluth, Timberland PRO, Rocky, Wrangler, Wolverine, Red Wing, Carhartt: work apparel and boots. Wolverine holds exclusivity as the work boot partner of the Paramount+ series Landman (season 2, per its record); Timberland PRO is Official Protective Footwear partner of NASCAR; Rocky is official footwear of Whitetails Unlimited. [VERIFIED per records; exclusivity terms for events UNVERIFIED]
- Northern Tool, Tractor Supply, Ace Hardware, Snap-on: tool and hardware retail overlap; Northern Tool runs a Monster Jam partnership, Snap-on a drag racing sponsorship.
- Bosch, Klein Tools, Snap-on, Northern Tool: tools. Bosch runs Super Bowl advertising; Klein is new to trade shows.
- Kubota vs John Deere vs Bobcat: Kubota is Official Partner of NASCAR (multi-year); Deere holds the John Deere Classic PGA Tour title through 2030; Bobcat sponsors the North Dakota Open.
- Polaris vs Yamaha vs BRP Can-Am: UTV and side-by-side.
- Ram vs Nissan: pickups. Ram is exclusive truck sponsor of America250.
- Pilot Flying J vs Love's: travel centers; both sponsor motorsports.
- Nationwide vs Travelers vs Liberty Mutual: insurance. State Farm runs a firefighter safety grants program with the NVFC that competes with Nationwide's fire-department space (source: Nationwide deep-pass record). Liberty Mutual's New York Liberty WNBA patch is a sponsorship, no fire-service tie.
- Goodyear vs Michelin vs Bridgestone: tires; Michelin holds the IMSA extension to 2035.
- 5.11 Tactical vs Under Armour tactical: gear (Under Armour failed the screen).

## Cross-category cautions
- Rocket Companies and Giant Eagle scored lower and are not in the 30; both sit on Cleveland Cavaliers assets, so avoid pairing them with each other.
- A trucking-school night can host Kenworth plus one travel center, but not both Pilot and Love's.
- Ohio events: FirstEnergy and Nationwide are both Ohio-rooted and do not conflict with each other.
